# 5 E-commerce Link Building Tactics

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
E-commerce product pages rarely attract links naturally, so build links to content and brand assets, then route equity to money pages with internal links. Five tactics: product-feedback pitches to listicle authors, guest post updates on outdated linked pages, unlinked mentions, manufacturer "where to buy" listings, and curated statistics pages.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Product feedback technique.** Find "best [product]" listicles that don't feature you; offer a free sample for feedback (never "product for link" — that's a link scheme). If they like it, ask to be considered for inclusion; if not, treat it as product feedback. Gifted products require disclosure, and any link given in return should be rel="sponsored" or nofollow. Precondition: your product must genuinely compete.
   - Find them: search `best [product category] -[yourbrand]`, pitch authors whose lists lack you.
2. **Guest post updates.** Offer editors to refresh their existing (outdated) post instead of pitching a new guest post — you can cherry-pick pages that already have backlinks, so your link is stronger.
   - Find prospects (content research tool): `"topic" -title:"topic" title:(2020 OR 2019 OR 2018 OR 2017 OR 2016 OR 2015)` (adjust to years that are now clearly stale) — pages mentioning your topic but not competing with it, with stale years in titles
   - Filters: referring domains ≥ 50 (linked page), domain authority ≥ 40 on the tool's scale (credible site)
   - Pitch angle: their traffic is declining and content is outdated; a refresh (with your contextual link) helps them
3. **Unlinked mentions.** Brand-mentioned-but-unlinked pages convert well: authors know you and often already like the product.
   - Find: search your brand in quotes → highlight unlinked domains → one page per domain → export (can yield thousands; filter by authority/traffic)
   - Pitch a contextually sensible destination (e.g., your materials/specs page they discussed), explaining the value for THEIR readers — not just "make it clickable"
4. **"Where to buy" / stockist pages.** Manufacturers list retailers that stock their products — free, relevant links if you sell third-party brands.
   - Find: `[brand name] intitle:("where to buy" OR "stockists")` per brand you stock; or scan competitors' backlinks filtered to pages with "where to buy"/"stockists" in titles
   - Pitch: short and obvious — "we stock and sell your products, can we be listed?"
5. **Curated statistics pages.** Journalists and bloggers search for stats to cite; publish the stats page for your niche.
   - Find keywords: keyword ideas around your product terms + "statistics, stats, facts, figures"
   - Validate: top-ranking pages for the query should have many backlinks (proof people cite these)
   - Publish, then do outreach; route the earned equity to product/category pages via internal links (middleman method)

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] List product categories; find 10 listicles missing you; send feedback pitches
- [ ] Run the outdated-content query for your core topics (RD ≥50, authority ≥40); pitch 5 updates
- [ ] Export unlinked mentions; filter; pitch contextual link additions
- [ ] List stocked brands; find their stockist pages; request inclusion
- [ ] Validate and publish one statistics page; build 20-30 links to it
- [ ] Internally link statistics/content pages → category and product pages

## Data points & benchmarks
- One statistics page built this way earned 37 backlinks with modest outreach
- Guest post update prospect filters: ≥50 referring domains to the page, ≥40 domain authority
- Unlinked-mention exports can run into the thousands of pages — always filter by authority/traffic
- Example guest-post-update target: an outdated (2019) list with 89 referring domains and declining traffic — ideal refresh pitch

## Mistakes to avoid
- Offering products explicitly in exchange for links — a documented link scheme
- Pitching guest POSTS when the page UPDATE gets you a stronger existing-linked page
- Generic "make the link clickable" asks; propose the specific contextually right page
- Building all links to the homepage and none to content that can pass equity onward
- Skipping statistics-page SERP validation (no backlinks to ranking pages = no cite demand)

## Key principles
- Easiest e-commerce links go to content and brand mentions, not product pages — then internal links move the equity
- Authors who already know/like your brand convert disproportionately
- Refreshing someone's decaying asset is a two-way-win pitch
- Manufacturers want buyers to find stockists — their pages are free link inventory
- Statistics pages turn your niche knowledge into a passive link magnet
