# 9 Easy Local Link Building Tactics

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Links are among the factors that influence local-pack rankings, and local competition is often modest — in one older study, first-page local businesses rarely exceeded ~150 referring domains. Do the unglamorous basics: listings on pages that already rank, competitor link overlap, lost-link reclamation, citations, locally relevant content, and community/sponsorship links.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Get links from other ranking sites ("barnacle SEO").** Search your money terms; the directories and aggregators ranking alongside competitors will list you — usually just by signing up. Instant consideration-set placement plus direct traffic.
2. **Get links your competitors have.** Run a link intersect (competitors vs. you): expect niche-relevant sites and lots of local directories/citations (name-address-phone listings). Claim them.
3. **Reclaim lost links.** Old URLs with backlinks die in site redesigns. Find them (top pages by links filtered to 404, sorted by referring domains) and 301 them to current equivalents.
4. **Create locally relevant content.** "Best grass for [area]", "common local pests", "[service] prices in [city]". Rarely attracts links naturally — plan to earn them via outreach.
5. **Get local citations.** Start with the major data aggregators and the big maps/review/business platforms (US examples; use your country's equivalents) since they feed data to hundreds of other sites, then expand to niche and city directories.
6. **Pursue other local opportunities:** colleges (job posts, scholarships, club sponsorships, discounts, alumni pages), city-specific sites/directories, local news/magazines/podcasts, state/regional sites, community groups, sponsorships (sports leagues, races, meetups, charities), local awards. Leverage existing relationships: supplier testimonials, case studies, business-group connections. Ego bait (local expert roundups) works well locally. Links given in exchange for a scholarship, discount, sponsorship, or donation are paid placements: expect or request rel="sponsored"/nofollow, and don't run scholarship programs just to farm links.
7. **Claim unlinked mentions.** Best in news-covered niches (e.g., legal) or when your team is active in the community.
8. **Acquire real businesses.** Rare but valuable: acquiring a genuine competitor or local business brings its customers, content, and links — either run both sites or consolidate with proper redirects. Buying expired or lapsed domains purely to pass ranking signals violates Google's spam policies; don't.
9. **Add internal links.** Your own site is a local site you fully control; internal links are the cheapest link tactic you have.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Search 5 money keywords; list every reputable directory/aggregator ranking on page 1; submit to each
- [ ] Run link intersect vs. 3-5 local competitors; claim citations and niche links
- [ ] Export 404s with referring domains; set up redirects
- [ ] Plan 3-5 locally relevant content pieces + outreach
- [ ] Complete listings on core aggregators + major maps/review platforms
- [ ] Contact 3 local institutions (college, chamber, league) about sponsorship/listing opportunities
- [ ] Monitor mentions; convert unlinked ones
- [ ] Watch for local businesses that are genuinely for sale (real operating businesses only, not expired domains)
- [ ] Add internal links from high-authority local pages to money pages

## Data points & benchmarks
- In one older study, first-page local businesses rarely had more than ~150 referring domains — check the backlink profiles of the top results for your query to size the gap
- Links often rank among the top factors in survey-based local/map-pack ranking studies (survey opinion, not proof)
- Local acquisitions are rare (a handful per career) but can be high-value when the business is real

## Mistakes to avoid
- Skipping basics to chase trendy tactics — riskier and rarely pays off locally
- Buying expired domains or paying for untagged local links — spam-policy violations
- Building links from non-local, non-relevant sites when local relevance is the point
- Ignoring citations because they're low-authority — they feed data to many other sites
- Forgetting internal links entirely even though they're fully within your control
- Not redirecting dead URLs after redesigns

## Key principles
- Local link building is a volume-of-basics game: directories, citations, reclaim, repeat
- Rank where your customers search: get listed ON the pages that rank for your terms
- Local relevance beats raw authority for map-pack performance
- Community involvement converts to links (sponsorships, schools, awards)
- You control one local site already — use its internal links fully
