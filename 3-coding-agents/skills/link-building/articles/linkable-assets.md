# 6 Linkable Asset Types (And EXACTLY How to Earn Links With Them)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
People rarely link to commercial pages — you need content deliberately built to attract links ("linkable assets"). Six proven types: infographics/graphics, online tools & calculators, awards & rankings, studies & research, definitive guides, coined terms. Assets don't attract links out of thin air — each type has its own promotion playbook.

## Framework / Step-by-step
People link because content: solves a problem, offers a unique angle, backs up their point, or interests their audience. Build these triggers into the asset.

**Type 1 — Infographics / GIFographics / Map-o-graphics**
- Only use when content genuinely benefits from visual format
- Earn links: (a) a content research tool: `[niche] "infographic"` → In title → last 12 months → one page per domain = sites that actively publish infographics (~130 for "SEO"); (b) port proven concepts from other niches ("Reddit's guide to fitness" → "...to SEO"); (c) infographic directories = weak nofollow links, only for brand-new sites
- Results examples: a well-known periodic-table-style SEO graphic (8.6K links/2.3K RDs), an interactive explainer from a graphics studio (21.7K links/1.3K RDs)

**Type 2 — Online tools & calculators**
- Highest ceiling (one headline-analyzer tool: 16K+ links/3.6K RDs; one color-palette tool: 154K links/5K RDs) but highest barrier (need dev skills/budget)
- Earn links: (a) pitch pages that already discuss the problem your tool solves (search the manual formula/process in Google/a content research tool → suggest adding your calculator); (b) low-cost social/PPC ads to build awareness → natural links follow; (c) resource page outreach

**Type 3 — Awards & rankings**
- Requires authority/credibility in the niche; hack: survey 100 industry influencers and rank by their votes (publish the methodology and rank honestly, no pay-to-win)
- Earn links: (a) notify every "winner" (they share → awareness → links; point them to their press/awards page — a high share will add it); (b) social ads targeting fans of the winners' pages
- Examples: a famous billionaire ranking (229K links/10.6K RDs), a remote-work city ranking site (36K/3.8K)

**Type 4 — Studies & research**
- Original data attracts links "like CRAZY" (4 of the 5 most-linked pages on one large SEO blog are studies); personal case studies work if you lack data
- Earn links: (a) re-conduct outdated studies and steal their links (one outdated ranking-factors study: 68K links/5K RDs, never updated — a content research tool: `[keyword] "study"/"survey"/"research"` sorted by RDs); (b) find pages asserting claims your data proves/disproves → pitch your study (worked: link added 2 days later)

**Type 5 — "Definitive" guides & tutorials**
- Experts only; bloggers/journalists prefer linking to ONE definitive resource
- Earn links: (a) update + re-promote on a schedule — email list (one brand used ~46K subscribers), a few hundred dollars of social ads, fresh outreach round each update → visible RD spike per refresh; (b) steal links from less-definitive guides: backlink tool → export referring domains of the weaker guides ranking for your topic → "just found your post, we published a more comprehensive guide" (no hard sell)

**Type 6 — Coined terms**
- Hardest to pull off, biggest passive payoff (one famous named technique still draws 50-100 new RDs/month years later)
- Earn links: (a) mention alerts for RELATED terms (people mentioning the term itself usually already link); (b) popularize the term via guest posts on high-traffic blogs (find via `author:"Name" -site:theirsite.com` in a content research tool)

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Pick asset type matching your resources (visual → infographic; budget/dev → tool; authority → awards; data → study; expertise → guide)
- [ ] Brainstorm by sorting a content research tool RDs desc within the asset format
- [ ] Build linking triggers (solution, unique angle, citable evidence) into the asset
- [ ] Choose 1-2 promotion plays from the type-specific playbook BEFORE publishing
- [ ] Schedule periodic updates + re-promotion for guides
- [ ] Notify every featured person/brand when awards/lists publish

## Data points & benchmarks
- Headline-analyzer tool: 16K+ links / 3.6K+ RDs; color-palette tool 154K+ links / 5K+ RDs (outliers, not typical)
- One outdated study: 68K+ links / 5K+ RDs ripe for reconduction
- Social ad spend per guide update in one case: $200-300
- One brand's email list: 46,000 subscribers used for re-promotion
- "SEO infographic" prospect search: ~130 active publisher sites in 12 months
- Famous coined-term page: 50-100 new RDs/month passively

## Mistakes to avoid
- Creating linkable assets without a promotion plan — "assets don't attract links out of thin air"
- Using infographics where the content doesn't benefit from visual format
- Running awards without existing credibility (or without the 100-expert-vote hack)
- Guest posting only for the byline link instead of popularizing your term
- Hard-selling in "better guide" outreach — inform, don't push

## Key principles
- Linkable assets have much higher ROI on promotion than commercial pages.
- Awareness is the first stepping stone to any link (winners share; ads build reach).
- Reconducting outdated studies is one of the highest-leverage link plays in data-rich niches.
