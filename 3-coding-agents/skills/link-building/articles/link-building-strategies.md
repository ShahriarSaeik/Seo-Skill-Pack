# Link Building Strategies That Work (12 Practitioners, 9 Tactics)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A practitioner roundup of tactics that were working for the practitioners interviewed: listicle gap-pitches, trend-jacking data campaigns, journalist relationships, map-o-graphics, passive-link statistics pages, creative giveaways, accessibility-swap outreach, AI-assisted guest posting, and a skyscraper revival. Common threads: exclusivity, real data, and value-led pitches instead of volume spam.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Pitch "best of" listicles that feature competitors but not you.** Search a content research tool for pages mentioning `[competitor 1]` and `[competitor 2]` but not `[your brand]`, with "best" or "top" in the title. Pitch elements: why you merit inclusion, other reputable lists already featuring you, a question about their criteria, and free product/tool access or case studies to evaluate (disclose any free access; never pay for inclusion without a sponsored/nofollow tag). One operator landed a client in nearly every top-ranking listicle for a competitive "best suppliers" query — brand exposure beyond the links themselves.
2. **Create data-led campaigns about a trending topic, pitch journalists.** Find trends via trend tools, news monitoring, and keyword growth metrics. Source unique data (government/statistical databases, FOI requests), publish journalist-ready, then pitch writers who covered the topic before (find via media databases, social search, or a content tool's recent-articles authors). One illegal-vape-hotspots investigation (FOI data): 72 referring domains from newspapers.
3. **Build journalist relationships, then respond to their requests.** Set keyword alerts for client-relevant terms; identify journalists writing 2+ recent relevant pieces; introduce yourself and offer exclusive commentary; always send a thank-you after coverage — it triggers repeat requests. Never send AI-written replies to journalists; use AI only for idea generation.
4. **Create map-o-graphics.** Pick a map-shaped topic where your brand has legitimate expertise (e.g., a cocktail brand mapping speakeasy bars). Validate against proven formats (e.g., a tourist-tax list with 325 referring domains). One lifetime-tax-by-state map: 188 referring domains.
5. **Rank statistics pages for "fact-finder" keywords.** Filter keyword matches for "statistics", "facts", "graph". Ideal SERP: link spread across several pages (3-4 pages with 100+ referring domains, a few with 20+) — not one monopoly. Win on content quality: charts up front, stats not buried in prose. One practitioner's stats assets average 102 referring domains each, with zero outreach; one page: 98 referring domains passively.
6. **Host unique giveaways/promotions, promote to journalists.** The crazier, the better: one brand paid $1,000 to binge-watch a sitcom for 15 hours → 150 referring domains from major media; another campaign secured 291 referring domains. Find prospects by exporting sites that linked to similar past promotions and pitching them directly. Follow local promotion/sweepstakes rules and disclose prizes; don't offer prizes in exchange for links.
7. **Pitch sites linking to resources with accessibility issues.** Find linked resources (100+ referring domains), run a free accessibility audit; if non-compliant, build a compliant alternative and tell linkers (including .gov and .edu sites) their linked resource fails impaired users. High-response outreach because it's genuinely helpful.
8. **Guest posting with AI assistance.** Prospect: content about your industry, authority roughly 30-60 (big sites rarely accept), sorted by traffic. Use AI for subject lines, pitch copy, and custom visuals — one practitioner's quirky AI-generated niche images got outsized attention. Mass generic pitches are dead; targeted creative ones still work. The posts themselves should be written and fact-checked by a person; mass-produced AI guest posts are a scaled-content and link-scheme risk.
9. **Skyscraper revival.** Find linked-but-outdated pages (e.g., a headphone list with 469 referring domains, untouched since 2021), publish the current version, pitch linkers as a replacement. Boost response by offering to share their content on your social channels.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Run the listicle-gap query for 2-3 competitor pairs; pitch with social proof + criteria question
- [ ] Set up trend monitoring (news + keyword growth) for your niche
- [ ] List 5 relevant government/NGO datasets + 3 FOI-able questions in your industry
- [ ] Build a journalist list: writers with 2+ relevant recent articles; introduce, offer exclusives
- [ ] Shortlist 3 map-o-graphic ideas where you have credible expertise
- [ ] Mine "statistics/facts" keywords; check SERP link spread before committing
- [ ] Export linkers of past viral promotions in your niche for giveaway pitches
- [ ] Audit one popular linked resource for accessibility; build the compliant replacement
- [ ] Refresh guest-post prospecting with AI-personalized pitches and custom visuals
- [ ] Find 5 linked-but-stale pages in your niche; skyscraper them with a social-share sweetener

## Data points & benchmarks
- FOI-based vape hotspots campaign: 72 referring domains
- Lifetime tax map: 188 referring domains; tourist-tax list inspiration: 325 referring domains
- Statistics-page program: ~102 referring domains average per asset, no outreach; single page: 98
- Binge-watching promo ($1,000 prize): 150 referring domains incl. major media; best giveaway result: 291 referring domains
- Stale skyscraper target example: 469 referring domains pointing at a 3-year-old list
- 1,952 tax-related pages with 100+ referring domains — linked-resource pool for accessibility outreach

## Mistakes to avoid
- Generic mass pitches — every tactic above works because the pitch is tailored or genuinely useful
- AI-written journalist replies — fastest route to a publication's ban list
- Trend-jacking without unique data — journalists need something new to cite
- Stats-page keywords where one page hoards all the links
- Guest-posting at authority extremes: big sites decline, junk sites don't pass value

## Key principles
- Link building and brand exposure compound: listicles, maps, and giveaways put you in front of buyers, not just crawlers
- Unique data (FOI, proprietary, merged sources) is the currency of digital PR
- Relationships convert: a thank-you note after coverage is a repeat-coverage investment
- Passive link assets (stats pages) remove outreach time from the equation
- Helpful, specific outreach (accessibility fixes, freshness swaps) outperforms asks at scale
