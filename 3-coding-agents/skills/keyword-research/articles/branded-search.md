# Branded vs. Non-Branded Search: Owning Your Brand Queries

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Branded searches contain your company or product name; non-branded searches don't. Non-branded SEO attracts strangers; branded SEO captures people already looking for you — buyers close to purchase, current customers, press, and fans. Optimize branded search by diffing "branded keywords you rank for" against "all branded keywords," then fixing underperformers, intent mismatches, and gaps with new pages plus internal links.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Build two lists** (country by country — repeat for every target market):
   - **Keywords you rank for**: your domain's organic keyword rankings (Search Console or a rank tracker), filtered to your brand and product words.
   - **All branded keywords**: enter your brand/product names as seeds in a keyword tool, pull the related/matching terms, export.
   - Diff them in a spreadsheet (a COUNTIF across the two lists flags "do we rank?" true/false).
   - Ignore misspellings — search engines autocorrect them once the correctly optimized page ranks.
2. **Pick keywords worth targeting** — three buckets:
   - **Underperforming**: anything not #1. Top 3 is the usual goal; for hotly contested comparison queries, top 10 counts as success. Sort by position, mark candidates, check volume.
   - **Mismatched intent**: you rank #1 but the content disappoints (classic example: searchers want an official, downloadable logo; the ranking page doesn't offer one).
   - **Valuable new keywords**: from the all-branded list where you don't rank — sort by volume; watch for "your brand vs competitor" queries.
3. **Optimize or create** — underperforming/mismatched → rework the existing page; new keywords → new page, or fold small topics into an existing page when one page serves intent better.
4. **Add internal links** — three placements: site navigation (top/footer); your most-trafficked or most-relevant pages (homepage, feature pages); and any page whose text already mentions the keyword (a site crawler's internal-link suggestions, or a site search for the keyword, will find these).

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Export ranked branded keywords and all branded keywords; diff in a spreadsheet
- [ ] Tag each keyword: underperforming / mismatched intent / new
- [ ] Fix positions 2–3 on branded terms with real volume; pursue #1 for your own name
- [ ] Audit #1 branded rankings for intent match (assets, comparisons, support content)
- [ ] Publish pages for "brand vs competitor" and common branded questions
- [ ] Link branded-content pages from navigation, high-traffic pages, and keyword-mention pages
- [ ] Re-run per country; track positions — branded SEO compounds but isn't overnight

## Data points & benchmarks
- Branded share of search: roughly 19% for small businesses vs. 42% for global brands (older study; treat as directional) — branded demand grows with the company
- Success thresholds: #1 for most branded queries; top 3 usually; top 10 for contested comparison queries

## Mistakes to avoid
- Dismissing branded SEO because "we'll rank for our name anyway" — competitors and review sites intercept comparison queries
- Optimizing misspelling variants instead of the canonical term
- Creating a new page for every branded long-tail when one consolidated page serves intent better
- Skipping the internal-link step and leaving branded pages orphaned
- Expecting instant movement; branded rankings follow normal SEO timelines

## Key principles
- The SERP for your brand name is your business card — control what searchers find.
- Branded searchers are the highest-intent traffic you'll ever get: they already know you.
- Diffing ranked vs. possible branded keywords turns brand equity into a concrete keyword list.
