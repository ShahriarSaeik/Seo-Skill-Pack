# Advanced Keyword Research: 5 Ways to Find Untapped Keywords

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Seed-and-sift keyword research surfaces the same keywords everyone else targets. Five advanced tactics find what competitors haven't: fresh pages already earning traffic, competitors' genuinely low-competition topics, SERP-altering modifiers, uncommon seed keywords, and at-scale "vs/review" affiliate keywords. Untapped = less competition, faster rankings.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Find newly published pages with traffic** — in a content explorer: broad topic "in title" + published in last 90 days + 500+ monthly search visits + site authority no higher than yours + exclude homepages/subdomains. A page winning traffic within days of publishing on a no-bigger site proves a rankable, fresh topic (examples: a "highest protein nuts and seeds" listicle hit ~1.1K visits/mo almost immediately; a muffin recipe hit 963 visits within two months via a KD 12 keyword). Caveat: some pages show short spikes (top-stories placements, briefly cracking a high-volume top 10) — always inspect the traffic source before committing.
2. **Steal competitors' low-competition topics** — competitor domain → Top pages → filter to traffic from keywords under KD 20 with 500+ visits. Then compare the page's TOTAL traffic vs its low-KD traffic: numbers close = truly low-competition topic; numbers far apart = high-competition page with incidental long-tail (skip it).
3. **Expand unique modifier keywords** — a useful modifier must (a) combine sensibly with popular niche keywords AND (b) CHANGE the SERP ("best credit cards" vs "+2022" → identical results; "+for bad credit" → completely different results). Mine competitor top pages for base+modifier patterns ("low carb mcdonalds," "low carb at red robin" → base "low carb" + restaurant modifier), then plug the base keyword into matching terms and Include-filter a list of other popular modifiers → dozens of low-competition topics to evaluate. Build a page only where it can offer unique substance; mass-producing near-duplicate templated pages risks scaled-content/doorway spam.
4. **Identify uncommon seeds** — competitors all research from the same common seeds, so those keyword pools are fought over. Take a popular niche site's organic keywords, EXCLUDE common seeds, and harvest odd-but-popular terms (fitness examples: "tdee," "russian twist," "bcaas"). Use them as seeds and filter low KD: decent volume, untouched competition.
5. **Find affiliate keywords at scale** — "best X" listicles aren't the only money keywords: "X vs Y" and "X review" attract buyers who've narrowed their choice — you're the last touchpoint before purchase. Disclose affiliate relationships and mark affiliate links rel="sponsored" (or nofollow). Method: enter popular niche brands into a keyword tool → matching terms → Include filter (any word): "review", "vs". Source brand lists from the brand filters of niche ecommerce stores.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Monthly: run the 90-days/500+visits/authority-no-higher-than-yours fresh-content scan in your niche
- [ ] Verify each candidate's traffic is stable and keyword-driven, not a spike
- [ ] For each competitor: pull top pages filtered to KD<20 traffic and compare total vs low-KD traffic
- [ ] Collect base+modifier patterns from competitor keywords; test that modifiers actually alter the SERP
- [ ] Exclude common seeds from a niche leader's keywords to build an uncommon seed list
- [ ] Batch-generate "vs"/"review" keywords from niche brand lists for affiliate content

## Data points & benchmarks
- Fresh-page filter recipe: last 90 days, 500+ monthly visits, site authority no higher than yours
- Example results: ~1.1K visits/mo within days (protein listicle); 963 visits/mo in 2 months (recipe, KD 12)
- Low-competition test: page's low-KD traffic ≈ total traffic
- Affiliate modifier filter: "vs" + "review" over brand seed lists

## Mistakes to avoid
- Chasing fresh pages with traffic spikes (news/top-stories flux) without checking the source
- Assuming a competitor page with big traffic is low-competition — check the KD split of that traffic
- Using modifiers that don't change the SERP (year suffixes on "best" queries)
- Researching from the same seeds as everyone else
- Limiting affiliate strategy to "best" listicles and ignoring vs/review intent

## Key principles
- Keywords competitors haven't found are inherently lower competition — being first beats being best on crowded terms.
- Proof beats prediction: pages that already rank fast on sites of equal authority are the safest bets.
- A modifier only creates a new opportunity if it creates a new SERP.
- Uncommon seeds are the cheapest untapped idea source in any mature niche.
