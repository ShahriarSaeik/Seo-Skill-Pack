# Niche Site Ideas: How to Evaluate and Mine a Niche

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A good niche site idea sits where sustained interest, keyword demand, and monetization overlap. The evaluation method is repeatable: pick a niche, mine its keyword patterns ("best X in [place]", "best X for Y", "X vs Y", "[product] review", questions), filter for low difficulty, verify a ranking opening in the SERP, and confirm monetization depth (commission rates, order values). Ten worked niches demonstrate the patterns.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Choose a niche you can sustain** — passion makes hundreds of posts easier, but validate with data before committing.
2. **Mine the niche's keyword patterns** — enter a broad seed in any keyword research tool and use include-filters to surface repeating patterns:
   - "best [product] in [location]" — local-intent lists, monetize with display ads
   - "best [product] for [user/need]" — high-ticket, person-specific, lower competition
   - "[brand A] vs [brand B]" — comparison posts for buyers who've narrowed their choice
   - "[product] review" — single-product reviews for near-purchase searchers
   - question keywords — informational content for display ads
   - Use wildcards (e.g., "kit*") where your tool supports them to catch plurals and variants of a pattern.
   - Templated patterns ("legal exotic pets in [state]") only work as pages if each one carries unique, genuinely useful information; near-duplicate templated pages risk doorway/scaled-content spam.
3. **Filter for winnable keywords** — KD under ~20 (lower for brand-new sites); weigh traffic potential, not just volume.
4. **Verify a SERP opening** — in the live SERP, look for at least one top-10 result from a lower-authority site; no dominant trusted review site in the niche = room to become the go-to brand.
5. **Check the trend trajectory** — prefer niches with flat or rising search interest (e.g., e-bikes climbed steadily).
6. **Validate monetization depth** — estimate average order value (bulk/kit purchases raise it); compare mass-marketplace programs (~3%) with specialist brand programs (5–16%); going direct to manufacturers often pays higher commissions. Disclose affiliate relationships and mark affiliate links rel="sponsored" (or nofollow).

## The ten worked niches (pattern highlights)
- Hiking — "best hikes in [location]" at scale; gear reviews; no dominant review site observed
- Survival — bulk/kit buying raises order value; 700+ keywords in one "best survival kit*" pattern
- E-bikes — rising trend, high price points (a ~$7,000 bike ≈ $280 commission at a 4% rate); brand "vs" comparisons; question content
- Pets — ~70% of households own pets; dogs/cats hyper-competitive → reptiles/exotics instead; "legal exotic pets in [state]" = ~50 pages from one pattern; ~9,000 keywords in the wider exotic-pet niche
- Golf — 230,000+ question keywords; 16,000+ courses → "best golf courses in [place]" scales endlessly
- Guitars — chord keywords are a low-competition goldmine; type-vs-type comparisons (some KD 0)
- Pressure washers — ~$2B global market; brand "vs"/"review" keywords with KD <10; featured-snippet opportunities
- Turntables — vinyl revival ($1B+ in a single market/year); "best turntable under [price]" keywords at KD ≤6
- Home gym — $6.4B+ equipment market; "best X for home gym" pattern; one machine type (smith machine) sustains 1,000+ exercise/workout keywords
- Work from home — ~92M remote workers needing supplies; "best [product] for working from home"; "how to pair [headphones]" questions → 7,000+ keywords

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Shortlist 2–3 niches you could write 100+ posts about
- [ ] For each, pull matching terms for the 5 core patterns and note keyword counts
- [ ] Apply a KD filter (under ~20; lower for brand-new sites) and confirm enough low-difficulty keywords exist
- [ ] Check the SERP for 2–3 money keywords: is any low-authority site ranking top 10?
- [ ] Estimate order values and commission rates (marketplace vs direct brand programs)
- [ ] Confirm the niche's search trend is flat or rising
- [ ] Widen or narrow scope based on Traffic Potential (e.g., one pet type → exotic pets)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Commission benchmarks (typical ranges; they vary by program): mass-marketplace programs ~3%; specialist brands 5–16%; education/software offers 25–60%
- Example low-KD finds: "best hiking gloves"; "reptile basics" (5,000 searches/mo, KD 0); turntable "under [price]" keywords KD ≤6
- E-bike economics: ~$7,000 product at a 4% program rate ≈ $280 per sale
- Market sizes (approximate, older figures; re-verify): pressure washers ~$2B; home gym equipment $6.4B+; vinyl $1B+ in one market/year
- Demand scale (a point-in-time snapshot): 230,000+ golf questions; ~38,000 home gym keywords; ~9,000 exotic pet keywords; 7,000+ headphone-pairing keywords

## Mistakes to avoid
- Choosing a niche on passion alone, without keyword and monetization data
- Competing head-on in saturated subtopics (dogs/cats) when adjacent ones (reptiles) are open
- Judging a niche by a single keyword's volume instead of pattern counts and Traffic Potential
- Sticking to marketplace affiliate programs when direct brand programs often pay more
- Missing the pattern multiplier: one "best X in Y" template suggests hundreds of page ideas across locations, states, price points, and user types — build only those pages that can carry unique, useful content

## Key principles
- A niche is a keyword mine: value = the number of winnable keyword patterns, not one big keyword.
- High average order value plus bulk/kit purchasing dramatically improves affiliate economics.
- The absence of a dominant, trusted review site is itself the opportunity.
- Patterns compound: location × product × price point × user type turns one template into a full content calendar.
