# Google AI Mode's Most-Cited Websites (Sept 2026 Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of broad US queries found Google AI Mode's citation mix mirrors AI Overviews — community and video platforms first (Reddit 17.9%, YouTube 17.8%) — but with even heavier Google self-citation (12.5% to its own properties) and a stronger commerce long tail. AI Mode is an agentic, multi-step experience, so sources that answer sub-questions in depth (forums, videos, product pages) get pulled repeatedly.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Reuse the AIO playbook with two adjustments** — Reddit edges YouTube at #1, and Google's own properties jump to #3 (12.5% vs 8.8% in AIOs), so Google-surface maintenance (Business Profile, Maps, Play/store listings, product feeds) matters more.
2. **Answer sub-questions, not just the seed query** — AI Mode runs multi-step research; content structured as self-contained answers per sub-question earns citations at multiple retrieval steps.
3. **Deepen commerce coverage** — Amazon (3.0%), Walmart (1.5%), eBay (1.1%), plus international Amazon storefronts appear; marketplace listings feed product research inside AI Mode.
4. **Cover the "second-tier evidence" set** — LinkedIn, Healthline, NYT, Edmunds, Business Insider, NerdWallet (~0.7-0.8% each) are the editorial layer when AI Mode needs authoritative validation.
5. **Track Reddit + YouTube as one program** — they're effectively tied at ~18% each; a combined community-video strategy addresses 36% of the citation economy.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] If AIO optimization exists, port it to AI Mode — source mixes largely overlap
- [ ] Audit and enrich Google-owned surfaces (Business Profile, store listings, shopping feeds)
- [ ] Structure key pages as Q&A blocks that standalone-answer fan-out sub-queries
- [ ] Maintain marketplace listings (Amazon primary, plus regional storefronts) as citation assets
- [ ] Keep Reddit and YouTube programs funded as the top-two shared priority (genuine, disclosed participation only)
- [ ] Monitor monthly rank movement in the cited-domains list for your niche

## Data points & benchmarks
- Top 10 by mention share: Reddit 17.9%, YouTube 17.8%, Google 12.5%, Facebook 10.2%, Instagram 5.7%, Wikipedia 4.0%, Quora 3.0%, Amazon 3.0%, TikTok 2.7%, Walmart 1.5%
- Reddit + YouTube = 35.7% combined; top 5 = 64.1%
- Pages cited: Google 2.96M, YouTube 1.55M, Reddit 1.2M — Google self-cites more pages than any external source
- Notable long tail: eBay 1.1%, Forbes 1.0%, Home Depot 0.9%, Healthline 0.8%, LinkedIn 0.8%, NYT 0.7%, Edmunds 0.7%, Business Insider 0.7%, NerdWallet 0.7%; multiple regional Amazon domains and cross-border shopping aggregators appear (0.3-0.9%)
- Low-authority outliers crack the list via sheer page volume (e.g., a low-authority shopping aggregator with ~20K cited pages)
- ChatGPT vs AIO vs AI Mode #1 source: Reddit (16.8%) vs YouTube (22.9%) vs Reddit (17.9%) — same platform family, different ordering; publisher share is small everywhere

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming AI Mode = AIOs exactly; self-citation and commerce weighting differ
- Neglecting Google-owned surfaces because "they're not your website" — they're 12.5% of citations
- Writing only for the original query instead of the sub-questions AI Mode decomposes it into
- Forgetting international/marketplace listings if you sell cross-border
- Skipping monthly monitoring — entrants like Trustpilot and new commerce domains rotate into the top 50 constantly

## Key principles
- AI Mode's agentic multi-step retrieval rewards content that answers many small questions well
- The citation economy across Google's AI surfaces concentrates in UGC + video + Google itself
- Marketplace and retail pages are major citation sources in agentic product research
- Where Google sends questions, Google also answers them: self-citation is structural, so optimize the surfaces Google controls
