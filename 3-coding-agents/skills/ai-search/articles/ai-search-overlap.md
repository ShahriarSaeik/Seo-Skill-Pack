# AI Citations vs Google Top 10: Only 12% Overlap (15K-Prompt Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study ran 15,000 long-tail prompts through Google, Bing, and four AI assistants: on average only 12% of AI-cited URLs rank in Google's top 10 for the same prompt (~10% for Bing), and ~80% don't rank for the original query at all. AI assistants select sources via fan-out sub-queries and rank fusion, not the literal SERP — so "rank #1" is not the citation strategy; owning query VARIATIONS is. (Google's AI Overviews are the exception: 76% of their citations come from the top 10.)

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Split your targets by surface** — AI Overviews: classic ranking still rules (76% from top 10). ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot: rankings barely predict citations (~6-8% overlap). Perplexity: middle ground, most SERP-aligned assistant (28.6%).
2. **Optimize for variation coverage, not single keywords** — a page ranking ~#6 across three query phrasings can beat a page ranking #1 for one (rank fusion rewards consistency across fan-out sub-queries).
3. **Map and target the full phrasing space** — cluster long-tail variants (parent topics, related terms, question forms); own as many permutations as possible with consolidated, comprehensive pages.
4. **Own the entity** — assistants personalize and synthesize; consistent brand/entity information across the web drives citation more than position.
5. **Measure per platform** — track citation overlap per assistant separately; a Google top-10 win guarantees little on ChatGPT and vice versa.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Pull the 20 highest-value prompts in your niche; list their phrasing variations
- [ ] Check your rankings across ALL variations (not just the head term) — consistency matters more than one #1
- [ ] Consolidate cannibalizing pages so one URL ranks across many variants
- [ ] For AI Overviews: keep investing in top-10 rankings (they translate)
- [ ] For ChatGPT/Gemini: invest in breadth of coverage + brand mentions instead of position chasing
- [ ] For Perplexity: maintain strong classic SEO — it's the most SERP-coupled assistant
- [ ] Re-audit quarterly; assistant index behavior shifts (providers and fallbacks change)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Avg AI citation overlap with Google top 10: 12% (ChatGPT in-text 8.0%, ChatGPT references 6.1%, Copilot 6.1%, Gemini 8.2%, Perplexity 28.6%)
- Avg overlap with Bing top 10: 10% (Copilot highest at 14% — it draws on Bing; ChatGPT ~8%; Perplexity 16.6%; Gemini 3.3%)
- >80% of assistant citations don't rank in Google for the original prompt (often not in top 100)
- AI Overviews: 76% of citations from Google top 10 — an extension of the SERP, unlike assistants
- Mechanism: fan-out sub-queries merged via reciprocal rank fusion; the literal user prompt may never be searched
- Study basis: 15,000 long-tail queries across Google + Bing + 4 assistants (one month's snapshot; re-check, as assistant behavior changes)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Reporting Google rank wins as evidence of AI visibility (88% of assistant citations come from outside the top 10)
- Building one page per exact-match keyword — fragmentation weakens fusion consistency
- Assuming all AI surfaces share one logic: AIOs ≈ SERP; assistants ≈ fan-out synthesis; Perplexity ≈ hybrid
- Ignoring query phrasing variants because they're "the same keyword"
- Expecting stable behavior: assistant search providers and fallbacks (Bing vs Google) keep changing

## Key principles
- AI Overviews follow the SERPs; AI assistants don't
- Citation selection rewards consistent presence across many sub-queries (fusion) over dominance of one
- Ranking still helps — but for queries you didn't plan, via variations you must map
- Perplexity is the most SEO-friendly assistant; ChatGPT/Gemini are the most SERP-independent
