# What Triggers AI Overviews? 86 Keyword Factors Across 146M SERPs

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 146 million SERPs (86 keyword traits; a snapshot, since AI Overview rates change often) found AI Overviews appear on ~20.5% of keywords overall — but that risk is highly concentrated: AI Overviews appear almost only on informational-intent keywords, and question-based, long-tail (7+ words), and YMYL (especially medical) queries trigger them at roughly 2-3x the base rate, while news, local, shopping, and navigational queries are largely safe. Use query traits to predict where AI Overviews will eat clicks and where they won't.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Score your keyword portfolio by AIO risk** using these trait multipliers vs. the 20.5% baseline: question format, 7+ words, informational/know intent, YMYL, reason/definition/short-fact phrasing → high risk. Newsy, local, shopping, NSFW, navigational/website, Do-intent → low risk.
2. **Protect high-risk informational traffic** — for queries likely to trigger AIOs, shift goals from click capture to citation capture (get cited inside the answer).
3. **Target low-AIO pockets for click-led strategy** — transactional/commercial, local, and fresh-news queries still largely render without AI answers; prioritize them where clicks matter most.
4. **Filter by SERP feature data** — use a keyword research tool's SERP feature filters to list which of your keywords currently trigger AI Overviews, then bucket by intent, length, and growth.
5. **Rebalance content plans by industry exposure** — science/health/pets/society content faces 35-44% AIO rates; shopping/real estate/sports/news faces 3-15%.

## Data points & benchmarks
- Baseline: AIOs on 20.5% of 146M SERPs (~30M keywords)
- Intent: nearly all AIO keywords carry an informational label (labels can overlap). Share of keywords of each intent that trigger an AIO: Informational 21.4%, Commercial 4.3%, Transactional 2.1%, Navigational 0.9%
- Branded vs not: AIOs on 24.9% of non-branded vs 13.1% of branded queries (1.9x)
- Query length: 1 word → 9.5%; 3 words → 14%; 5 words → 27.6%; 7+ words → 46.4%
- Questions: 57.9% of question queries trigger AIOs vs 15.5% of non-questions
- YMYL: 34.3% of YMYL queries trigger vs 17.2% non-YMYL; Medical 44.1%, Safety 31%, Other 31.5%, Legal 23.6%, Financial 22.9%
- Hardest-hit industries: Science 43.6%, Health 43%, Pets & Animals 36.8%, People & Society 35.3%, Internet & Telecom 30.3%, Reference 30.1%, Computers & Electronics 28.6%
- Safest industries: Shopping 3.2%, Real Estate 5.8%, Sports 14.8%, News 15.1%
- Query classifier rates: reason 59.8%, boolean 57.4%, definition 47.3%, consequence 46.7%, short fact 41.2%, instruction 35.1%, comparison 26.2%
- Very newsy queries: only 6.3% trigger AIOs (AIOs favor evergreen)
- Local searches: 7.9% trigger AIOs vs 22.8% non-local; NSFW: 4%
- CPC shows no clear correlation with AIO appearance (all buckets within ~±8pts of baseline)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming AI Overviews affect all keywords equally when planning defenses
- Ignoring AIO exposure on medical/health content — it's the single most affected vertical
- Writing off AI Overviews because your transactional pages "never see them" — your informational cluster does
- Chasing AIO citations on newsy or local queries where AIOs rarely render
- Treating branded queries as safe — 13.1% triggered AIOs in this study

## Key principles
- AI Overviews are an informational-query phenomenon: know > do > visit-in-person > website
- Longer, more specific queries = higher AIO probability (complexity signals a need to synthesize)
- YMYL caution does not suppress AIOs — medical queries get AI answers at 2x the base rate
- Query-trait screening is a practical forecasting tool for where AI answers will absorb traffic
