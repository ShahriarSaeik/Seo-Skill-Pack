# How to Rank on ChatGPT: What Actually Works (Based on Data)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
You can't "rank #1" in ChatGPT — responses are probabilistic (<1% chance of the same brand list twice across 100 asks). Instead, increase brand visibility (share of relevant prompts mentioning you) via 7 data-backed tactics: YouTube presence, web brand mentions, authentic Reddit participation, LLM-friendly content structure, freshness, quality over quantity, and crawler access.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Optimize YouTube presence** — strongest correlation with ChatGPT visibility (75K brand study). YouTube is LLM training data (GPT-4 was reportedly trained on 1M+ hours of YouTube transcripts) and LLMs must retrieve videos (not generate them). Mentions = brand in channel name, title, description, transcript, or link. Publish videos and work with creators (disclose paid sponsorships).
2. **Build brand mentions across the web** — next strongest factor; mentions ≠ backlinks. LLMs reflect consensus across multiple authoritative sources. Get onto sites ChatGPT already cites (find them with a cited-domains view or manual prompts), pitch "best of" list creation to authoritative sites lacking one (bring keyword data, competitive traffic data, product access; disclose any free access or affiliation), close mention gaps (pages mentioning competitors but not you). Never pay for undisclosed placements.
3. **Engage authentically on Reddit** — Reddit was ChatGPT's #1 most-cited domain in one study (847K mentions vs Wikipedia's 432K). Build reputation first: answer questions without pitching; mention your product only when directly relevant, disclose your affiliation, and follow each subreddit's rules (undisclosed promotion gets removed and damages the brand); look for subreddits where your topic fits naturally. Long game: months.
4. **Structure content for LLM extraction** — BLUF format; definitive language; atomic paragraphs; question-based headings; FAQ sections; name relevant entities, including competitors where useful (entity density); balance facts with "analyst voice" (subjectivity ~0.47/1).
5. **Keep content fresh** — substantive updates, not date changes: replace stats, update screenshots, revise recommendations, add new sections.
6. **Don't chase quantity** — page count has near-zero correlation with ChatGPT visibility. Publish original research with specific, quantified, citable claims instead.
7. **Ensure technical access** — check robots.txt for GPTBot/OAI-SearchBot blocks; check your CDN's AI-bot / managed robots.txt settings (some are enabled by default on many accounts).

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Search YouTube for your brand name to find tagged and untagged mentions; use an AI visibility tracker or manual prompts to track the rest
- [ ] Identify citable domains in your niche (a cited-domains view or manual prompts)
- [ ] Claim/optimize review platform profiles; ask real customers for honest reviews (never fake or incentivized-without-disclosure)
- [ ] Run a mention gap analysis (pages mentioning competitors but not you) → outreach to those pages
- [ ] Check which competitor review/comparison URLs get AI citations in a backlink analysis tool to prioritize
- [ ] Spend 20+ min/week authentically answering questions in relevant subreddits
- [ ] Rewrite key pages BLUF-style with question headings + FAQ blocks
- [ ] Schedule substantive updates of high-value pages (stats, screenshots, recommendations)
- [ ] Audit robots.txt + CDN/WAF AI-bot settings for crawler blocks

## Data points & benchmarks
- <1 in 100 chance ChatGPT returns the same brand list twice (industry experiment, 100x asks)
- Reddit = #1 cited domain in ChatGPT in one study: 847,338 mentions; Wikipedia 431,710; Amazon 97,457
- "Best of" blog lists = 43.83% of page types cited in ChatGPT responses for recommendation-type queries
- Citation distribution "ski ramp": 44.2% from first 30% of content; middle 30-70% = 31.1%; final third = 24.7%
- Within paragraphs, 53% of citations come from the middle sentence (highest information gain)
- Question-mark headings cited at 18% vs 8.9% (2x rate)
- Entity density of cited text: 20.6% (~3-4x normal prose)
- Subjectivity score of cited content: ~0.47 on 0-1 scale
- In one study, ChatGPT cited URLs averaging 393 days newer (in-text) and 458 days newer (citations) than Google organic results; overall AI-cited content is 25.7% fresher
- Definitive language in cited text: 36.2% vs 20.2%
- GPT-4 reportedly trained on 1M+ hours of YouTube transcriptions (press report)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Overt or undisclosed promotion on Reddit — the community is "allergic to marketing" and it backfires
- Faking freshness by changing publish dates without substantive updates (may be detected)
- Scaled low-quality content — AI platforms may add quality signals like Google's Panda
- Blocking AI crawlers accidentally (esp. CDN default AI-blocking features) — you lose control of your narrative in training data
- Pitching writers to squeeze into existing listicles instead of pitching new content creation

## Key principles
- "You can't rank #1 in ChatGPT, but you can increase your visibility — the percentage of relevant queries where your brand gets mentioned at all."
- LLMs look for consensus: multiple authoritative sources describing your brand as a strong solution
- Brands in AI responses are the ones being talked about on YouTube, review sites, Reddit, industry coverage
- Fresh + relevant pages with weak traditional rankings can still get cited
