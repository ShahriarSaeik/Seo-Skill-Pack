# AI Visibility Audit: How to Measure Your Brand's Presence in AI Search

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An 8-step repeatable audit (monthly/quarterly) measuring where, how often, how accurately, and from which sources your brand appears in AI search. Covers scope definition, benchmarking, response accuracy/sentiment analysis, topic associations, top-cited pages, influential mentions, competitor gaps, and turning findings into Fix/Build/Influence actions.
## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Define scope** — platforms (AIOs, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot), entities (main brand, products, features, metrics, people, sub-brands), regions/languages. Consistent scope = comparable data over time.
2. **Benchmark visibility** — manual free checks (customer questions across platforms, monthly/quarterly) OR an AI visibility tracker for consistent metrics: mentions, citations, impressions, AI share of voice, plus branded search demand (volume + query diversity trends).
3. **Analyze branded responses (accuracy + sentiment)** — filter tracked responses three ways: queries containing your brand (branded searches), responses containing your brand (general framing), and citations containing your domain (your content as source). Assess: accuracy, sentiment/framing, differentiation (unique attributes present?), authority (lead mention?), CTA (recommended next step?). Log misinformation: queries, responses, cited sources.
4. **Unbranded/topic analysis** — filter to queries that don't contain your brand and review the topics view = current associations. Then look at topic-only prompts, excluding your brand from query/response/citations → find missing sub-themes, formats AI cites, competitor-only topics. Close the gaps with new or expanded pages.
5. **Top-cited pages** — in your tracker, attach your domain to your brand entity (usually required for citation data), then list the cited pages on your domain. Cross-check with web analytics AI traffic: cited ≠ clicked; find queries/responses driving actual clicks.
6. **Best brand mentions** — shortcut: filter responses that mention your brand → their cited pages = the third-party pages shaping how AI describes you (e.g., listicles + Reddit). Use to: spot best mentions, trace misinformation sources, see winning formats, find partnership targets, learn competitive positioning.
7. **Competitor comparison** — add competitors. Compare three views: your brand only, head-to-head topics, and topics where only competitors appear (your gaps). Check SOV per topic; review cited domains/pages → sites mentioning competitors more than you.
8. **Strategy + reporting** — prioritize Fix (misinfo/outdated) / Build (missing topics/formats) / Influence (relationships with cited sites). Write up a stakeholder report; repeat monthly (or quarterly).

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Document entity list (legal names, abbreviations, products, people, sub-brands)
- [ ] Record baseline: mentions, citations, impressions, SOV per platform
- [ ] Review branded responses for accuracy/sentiment; log all errors + sources
- [ ] Run topic-only analysis excluding brand; list missing associations
- [ ] Pull top-cited pages; cross-reference with AI traffic pages
- [ ] Identify 10 most influential third-party mention pages; verify how they describe you
- [ ] Run a competitor gap analysis (topics where only competitors appear) per priority platform
- [ ] Build Fix/Build/Influence action plan; score by traffic, authority, AI visibility impact
- [ ] Write the stakeholder report; schedule next audit

## Data points & benchmarks
- Key metrics: Mentions, Citations, Impressions (exposure estimate), AI Share of Voice, branded search demand (volume + query count)
- Search demand interpretation: rising volume = more people seeking brand; rising query count = wider range of questions (brand-level → product/feature)
- "Mentioned but not cited" pattern = AI sources your brand info from competitors/third parties, not you
- Recommended cadence: monthly ideally, quarterly minimum

## Mistakes to avoid
- Comparing across inconsistent scopes/platforms/entities between audits
- Only tracking the main brand — sub-brands, products, executives have separate visibility profiles
- Treating citations as success without checking clicks (cited ≠ clicked)
- Skipping qualitative analysis (accuracy, sentiment, differentiation) for volume metrics only
- One-time audit — this must be a recurring cycle

## Key principles
- An audit measures volume AND qualitative representation (how AI frames you)
- The pages AI cites in responses mentioning you ARE your de facto brand messaging
- Gaps close through three actions only: Fix, Build, Influence
- Different patterns emerge per platform — analyze each separately
