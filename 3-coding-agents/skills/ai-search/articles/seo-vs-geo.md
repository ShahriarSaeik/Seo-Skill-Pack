# SEO vs GEO: 5 Differences, 4 Shared Foundations

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
SEO earns a link that must be clicked; GEO earns a mention inside the answer — often on a third-party site, often with zero clicks. The two share foundations (content quality, intent, trust, technical health), but they differ in user journey, metrics, mention sources, and traffic quality. Correct sequencing: build SEO as the foundation (still ~40% of site traffic vs ~0.2% from assistants), then layer GEO tactics on top.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Difference 1 — Link vs mention**: SEO output = a clickable blue link. GEO output = your brand/facts embedded in the answer text; source links are smaller, contextual, and rarely used.
2. **Difference 2 — Click journey vs zero-click**: with an AI summary present, users click any result only 8% of the time (vs 15% without), just 1% click the AI summary's sources, and 26% end the session entirely (vs 16%). Design for the answer, not the visit.
3. **Difference 3 — Click economics vs visibility economics**: SEO KPIs = rankings, backlinks, organic traffic, share of voice. GEO KPIs = AI mentions, citations, AI referral traffic, AI share of voice.
4. **Difference 4 — Your domain vs third parties**: most AI mentions of a brand originate on other sites (best-of lists, reviews, Reddit, PR). In one example, the top pages mentioning a brand in AI Overviews didn't include the brand's own site at all — unlinked mentions count. GEO is therefore interdisciplinary (PR, communities, reviews), not just site optimization.
5. **Difference 5 — Browsing vs intent traffic**: AI referrals view fewer pages (4 vs 5.2), stay longer (86s vs 78s), bounce more (67.8% vs 63.7%), land on homepages/product pages/free tools (~80% combined), and can convert far better (one SaaS: 0.5% of traffic → 12%+ of signups, ~23x).
6. **Sequence: SEO first, GEO second** — SEO still drives the overwhelming majority of traffic and provides the index/training base AI draws from; GEO future-proofs as zero-click grows.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Keep funding core SEO (content, links, technical) — it feeds GEO
- [ ] Add a visibility-measurement layer (mentions, citations, AI share of voice) alongside click KPIs
- [ ] Build third-party mention programs: best-of lists, review platforms, PR, Reddit
- [ ] Prioritize conversion-ready pages (product, pricing, tools) for AI arrivals
- [ ] Publish citable assets: original data, comparisons, statistics with dates
- [ ] Track branded search volume as the bridge metric connecting GEO influence to SEO demand
- [ ] Report SEO and GEO as one strategy with two measurement systems

## Data points & benchmarks
- With AI summaries: 8% click any result vs 15% without (~50% fewer clicks); 1% click AI-summary sources; 26% end session vs 16% (survey research, 2025)
- Traffic split benchmark: Google ≈ 41% of site traffic vs assistants ≈ 0.21% (Jan-Aug 2025, 50K+ sites)
- AI visitor behavior: 4 pages/visit vs 5.2; 86s vs 78s time on page; 67.8% vs 63.7% bounce
- AI traffic destination mix: free tools 36.5%, product pages 23.1%, homepage 20.4%
- Conversion: one brand's AI traffic = 0.5% of visits but 12%+ of signups (~23x); uplift varies by site (independent studies show mixed results)
- Trust: ~3 in 10 users trusted a leading assistant more than search engines; 36% had discovered a new brand/product through it (2025 survey)
- Branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview presence (correlation, not proof of cause); top-quartile mention brands earn 10x+ more placements than the next tier
- Quality content compounds: one site earned 7,470 mentions across 2,309 pages with zero dedicated "AI optimization"

## Mistakes to avoid
- Abandoning SEO metrics for AI metrics before AI sends meaningful traffic
- Judging GEO by clicks — the model is visibility, trust, and assisted demand
- Optimizing only your own site when most mentions come from third parties
- Sending AI-assisted visitors to thin pages when they arrive with high intent
- Treating SEO and GEO as competing budgets instead of layered investments

## Key principles
- SEO = click economics; GEO = visibility economics
- Zero-click is the default AI-search outcome; influence happens inside the answer
- Trust signals transfer: backlinks/EEAT for search, unlinked mentions/brand authority for AI
- The same content strengths win both — GEO is an extension of SEO, not a replacement
