# The Most-Cited Websites in Grok (Industry Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 1.9M US queries found Grok's citation economy is social-first: Reddit (16.3%), YouTube (15.1%), and Facebook (13.9%) dominate, with Quora unusually strong. Grok reportedly reaches ~117M monthly users (an estimate) through its X integration, so UGC and social platform presence is the lever, not traditional publishing.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Mention share = each domain's slice of all citations earned by the top sources. Grok's top-10 attention splits into:
1. **UGC/social platforms (the majority of top-source share)** — Reddit 16.3%, YouTube 15.1%, Facebook 13.9%, Instagram 5.9%, Quora 5.5%, TikTok 4.8%, X 1.4% (climbing +15 spots)
2. **Encyclopedic reference** — Wikipedia 3.4% only (much weaker than on other engines)
3. **Retail** — Amazon 5.0%, eBay 1.5%, Walmart 1.5%
4. **Vertical review/consumer** — Consumer Reports, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Trustpilot, KBB, Edmunds
5. **Health/science authority** — PubMed/PMC, Healthline, WebMD, ScienceDirect

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Prioritize Reddit and YouTube programs for Grok visibility above publisher outreach
- [ ] Add Quora to the answer-platform mix (unusually strong on Grok vs other assistants)
- [ ] Maintain an active X presence — its citation share jumped in one month (may not persist)
- [ ] For retail brands: complete marketplace listings (Amazon first) since marketplaces outrank brand sites
- [ ] Don't over-invest in Wikipedia for Grok specifically; its share is roughly half what it is on other engines
- [ ] Track mention share monthly — rankings reshuffle (new entries and double-digit movers every month)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Grok monthly users: ~117M (estimate; of X's ~550M MAU)
- Top shares: Reddit 16.3%, YouTube 15.1%, Facebook 13.9%, Instagram 5.9%, Quora 5.5%, Amazon 5.0%, TikTok 4.8%, Wikipedia 3.4%
- Breadth: Reddit ~1.85M distinct pages cited; YouTube ~1.96M; Facebook ~1.7M
- X.com at 1.4% after climbing 15 positions in one month
- Car-research (KBB, Edmunds, CarMax, Carfax, Cars.com) and home (Home Depot, Lowe's, Houzz, The Spruce) verticals all place in the top 50

## Mistakes to avoid
- Porting a Google-oriented publisher/PR strategy to Grok unchanged — its source mix skews social
- Ignoring Q&A platforms (Quora) that other engines under-weight
- Treating citation rank as stable; monthly volatility is high

## Key principles
- Each assistant has a distinct "source diet" — Grok's is social/UGC-heavy, reflecting its X roots.
- Broad citation breadth (1M+ pages) means platform presence, not single-page optimization, drives share.
- Distribution decides influence: an assistant embedded in a social network cites the social web.
