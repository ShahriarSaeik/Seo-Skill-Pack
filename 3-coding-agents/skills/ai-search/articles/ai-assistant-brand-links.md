# Mentions vs Citations: AI Assistants Linked One Brand's Mentions Only ~28% of the Time

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
In one brand's data (31K+ mentions), AI assistants included a link only ~28% of the time on average (10-52% depending on platform); treat this as a single-brand example, not a universal rate. But mentions that DO include links cluster on high-volume queries, so impression-weighted visibility is far higher than raw link rates suggest. Track mentions, citations, AND volume-weighted impressions — and use search volume as your proxy for prompt popularity.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Separate three metrics:** Mentions (brand named), Citations (your site linked), Impressions (mentions weighted by search volume of the prompt).
2. **Measure your citation rate per platform:** pull brand mentions, exclude your own domain's citations, divide linked by total. Repeat per assistant — rates differ by up to 5x.
3. **Weight by impressions:** recalculate citation share using prompt search volume. Linked mentions concentrate where demand is highest, so true visibility usually beats the raw link rate.
4. **Invest where volume lives:** search volume is the best available proxy for prompt popularity — high-volume keywords in traditional search map to the most common AI prompts (including their fan-out variations).
5. **Cover topics comprehensively:** assistants run multiple related sub-queries per prompt; thorough topic coverage gets you cited across variations you can't predict individually.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Report mention rate, citation rate, and impression-weighted citation share separately
- [ ] Benchmark citation rate per assistant; don't blend platforms
- [ ] Prioritize high-volume topics — small link percentages across thousands of queries compound
- [ ] Exclude own-domain citations when measuring third-party-driven visibility
- [ ] Re-check rates quarterly; citation behavior shifts with product updates

## Data points & benchmarks
- One brand, 31K+ mentions across a 150M-prompt database — linked-mention rates:
  - Lowest (Google AI Overviews): 10.7%
  - Gemini: 16.8% | Copilot: 26.1% | ChatGPT: 26.9% | AI Mode: 36.8%
  - Highest (Perplexity): 51.6%
- Same data weighted by impressions (share of people seeing linked mentions):
  - Perplexity 78.4% | Gemini 71.3% | AI Mode 46.2% | ChatGPT 36.6% | Copilot 31.7% | AI Overviews 13.0%

## Mistakes to avoid
- Judging AI visibility by link rate alone — unlinked mentions still build brand awareness
- Comparing platforms on blended averages — behavior differs by 5x
- Ignoring low-link platforms: even 10% linkage on huge-volume prompts yields meaningful exposure
- Chasing citations on long-tail prompts while ignoring high-volume heads

## Key principles
- Mention ≠ citation ≠ traffic: each layer converts at a different rate, per platform.
- Linked mentions skew toward high-volume queries — SEO demand research doubles as AI prompt research.
- A 20-30% citation rate across thousands of high-volume prompts is a large absolute visibility engine.
- You can't control a platform's citation behavior; you can control which topics you're a credible answer for.
