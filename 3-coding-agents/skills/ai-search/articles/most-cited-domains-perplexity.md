# The Most-Cited Websites in Perplexity (Industry Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 3.1M US queries shows Perplexity's citation economy is UGC-dominated and editorially diverse: Reddit (21.6%) and YouTube (20.8%) take over 40% of top-source attention between them, then Wikipedia, consumer media, health authorities, and product-review publishers share a long tail. Perplexity linked more generously than other assistants in this study (~52% of brand mentions came with links), so earning its citations pays double.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Mention share = each domain's slice of top-source citations. Perplexity's source mix:
1. **UGC platforms (~47% of top-source citations)** — Reddit 21.6%, YouTube 20.8%, Facebook 4.5%
2. **Reference** — Wikipedia 6.3%
3. **Commerce** — Amazon 3.6%, Alibaba 2.0%, Target, Etsy, Best Buy, eBay
4. **Health authorities** — Healthline 2.6%, WebMD, Medical News Today, Cleveland Clinic, PubMed/PMC (unusually strong)
5. **Consumer/editorial media** — Good Housekeeping, NYT, Forbes, Business Insider, TechRadar, CNET, PCMag, Tom's Guide, RTINGS
6. **Vertical research** — Edmunds, KBB, CarGurus, Cars.com (auto); Tripadvisor, Yelp (local); NerdWallet, Bankrate-style finance

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Prioritize Reddit threads and YouTube coverage — together they hold ~42% of top-source attention
- [ ] Publish original data and product testing: review sites (RTINGS, Consumer Reports) and product-testing media earn citations
- [ ] Health brands: align with clinical sourcing conventions (studies, medical reviewers) — Perplexity leans on medical authorities
- [ ] Keep Wikipedia accurate and current for entity grounding
- [ ] Since Perplexity actually links out, optimize cited pages for the click: clear next steps, unique value beyond the answer
- [ ] Track share monthly; big swings are normal (one publisher climbed 28 spots in a month)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Perplexity scale: hundreds of millions of queries monthly and tens of millions of active users (company-reported, older figures)
- Top shares: Reddit 21.6%, YouTube 20.8%, Wikipedia 6.3%, Facebook 4.5%, Amazon 3.6%
- Breadth: YouTube ~1.06M distinct pages cited; Reddit ~892K
- Long tail is thin: #10 (NYT) holds just 1.6% — attention is steep and UGC-heavy at the top
- Health vertical: 5+ medical authority domains in the top 45
- Review/testing sites (RTINGS, OutdoorGearLab-style) outrank general news for product queries

## Mistakes to avoid
- Treating Perplexity like a publisher-only channel — its top-2 sources are platforms, not publications
- Ignoring product-review and testing formats when Perplexity answers purchase queries
- Assuming citation stability — monthly rank swings of 10-30 positions are routine

## Key principles
- Perplexity is the "answer engine first" assistant: citations are the product, so source diversity is wider and the long tail matters.
- UGC (Reddit + YouTube) is the gateway; editorial review content converts citations for commercial queries.
- Because Perplexity linked at the highest rate of any assistant studied, visibility here converts to measurable traffic better than elsewhere.
