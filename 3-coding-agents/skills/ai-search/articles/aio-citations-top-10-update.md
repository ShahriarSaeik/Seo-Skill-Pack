# AI Overview Citations Pulling From the Top 10 Dropped to 38% (Industry Study Update)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A re-run of the citation-overlap study (863K keyword SERPs, 4M AI Overview URLs; a point-in-time snapshot) found that only ~38% of AI Overview citations now also rank in the top 10 of the same query's SERP — down from ~76% eight months earlier. Ranking for the exact query no longer guarantees citation; AI Overviews increasingly pull sources from fan-out sub-query SERPs and non-ranking surfaces, with YouTube the biggest winner.

## Framework / Step-by-step
What changed and why it matters:
1. **The overlap collapsed** — top-10 same-query overlap fell from ~76% to ~38%; the rest of the citations split almost evenly between positions 11–100 (~31%) and pages outside the top 100 (~31%)
2. **Cause: aggressive query fan-out** — newer answer models expand queries into more/broader sub-queries and cite pages that rank in those sub-SERPs rather than the original one
3. **SERP-feature sources count** — some cited pages appear only in features (video packs, discussions), not blue links; blue-link-only analysis pushes even more citations "outside" the top 100 (36.7%)
4. **YouTube is the structural exception** — among cited pages not ranking in the top 100, 18.2% were YouTube URLs; YouTube is now the most-cited domain in AI Overviews (+34% in six months) despite rarely ranking in classic results

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Stop benchmarking only against the page-1 top 10; include fan-out sub-topic pages, video results, and AIO citation winners as your real competitor set
- [ ] Map parent topics and cover sub-angles so you rank in fan-out SERPs, not just the head query
- [ ] Study fan-out expansions from any assistant at scale to find recurring sub-themes and intent angles (exact mirroring isn't possible or necessary)
- [ ] Add YouTube to the AI strategy: transcripts, titles, descriptions feed citations even without classic rankings
- [ ] Run "mentioned, not cited" gap analysis: find prompts where you appear but aren't the source, then reinforce that coverage
- [ ] Reverse-engineer cited formats (definitions, stats, step-by-steps) and strengthen ranking-but-uncited pages

## Data points & benchmarks
- 37.9% of AIO-cited URLs appear in the first 10 SERP blocks (incl. features); 37.1% in blue-links-only analysis
- ~31% of citations rank 11–100; ~31–37% rank outside the top 100
- Prior study (8 months earlier): ~76% top-10 overlap — the dependency on the original SERP halved
- YouTube: 18.2% of non-ranking citations; 5.6% of all AI Overview URLs; most-cited AIO domain, +34% in six months
- YouTube mentions correlate ~0.74 with AI Overview brand visibility (75K-brand study)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Equating a #1 ranking with an assumed citation — selection happens across fan-out SERPs
- Optimizing a single head-term page instead of cluster-wide sub-topic coverage
- Ignoring video and community surfaces because they don't "rank" in classic search
- Benchmarking content only against page-1 competitors when citation winners often sit elsewhere

## Key principles
- You now optimize for the journey (fan-out), not the keyword.
- Citation selection and organic ranking are decoupling fast; treat them as separate competitions.
- Pages ranking but not cited signal a coverage/clarity gap versus the expanded intent.
