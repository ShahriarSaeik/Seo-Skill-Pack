# Google AI Overviews' Most-Cited Websites (Sept 2026 Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 3M+ US queries found AI Overviews' citation economy is dominated by social, video, and community platforms — YouTube (22.9%), Reddit (18.5%), Facebook (10.1%), Google's own properties (8.8%), Instagram (5.6%), Quora (4.7%) — with classic web publishers far behind. AIOs reach 2B+ monthly users across 200+ countries, so these placements carry unmatched audience. Winning AIO citations means feeding the platforms Google already favors: video, community threads, and social.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Prioritize video first** — YouTube is the single most-cited source (22.9%, 1.7M+ distinct cited pages). Build topic-mapped video coverage, not one-off uploads.
2. **Invest in community presence** — Reddit (#2) and Quora (#6) threads are primary evidence; participate genuinely (and disclose your affiliation) in the subreddits and questions your buyers ask; undisclosed promotion backfires.
3. **Don't ignore mainstream social** — Facebook and Instagram hold 15.7% combined; public social content (posts, reels, discussion) gets surfaced in AI answers.
4. **Handle Google's self-citation (8.8%)** — maintain Google-owned surfaces (Business Profile, Maps data, product listings) since Google cites its own properties heavily.
5. **Keep the classic web play** — Wikipedia (#7), Amazon (#9), and retail domains (Walmart, Home Depot, Target, Best Buy, Lowe's, eBay) anchor commerce queries; publisher sites (Forbes, Healthline, WebMD, NYT, CNET) hold ~0.3-0.8% each.
6. **Compare per-platform source mixes** — AIOs ≠ ChatGPT: video/social lead here vs Reddit/publications on ChatGPT. Track each platform's cited set separately.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Publish YouTube videos mapped to your informational query clusters (titles = question queries)
- [ ] Run an ongoing, genuine and disclosed Reddit/Quora participation program in your niche
- [ ] Keep public Facebook/Instagram content indexable and keyword-aware
- [ ] Complete and maintain Google Business Profile + product feed data
- [ ] Verify/enhance Wikipedia coverage of your brand/entity where genuinely notable
- [ ] For e-commerce: optimize Amazon + retailer listings (they are citation sources, not just sales channels)
- [ ] Track your niche's AIO-cited domains monthly; shift effort toward movers

## Data points & benchmarks
- Top 10 by mention share: YouTube 22.9%, Reddit 18.5%, Facebook 10.1%, Google 8.8%, Instagram 5.6%, Quora 4.7%, Wikipedia 4.0%, TikTok 3.3%, Amazon 3.3%, Walmart 1.0%
- Top 2 sources (YouTube + Reddit) = 41.4% of top-50 citations; social/UGC/video platforms hold ~6 of the top 8 slots
- Long tail: Home Depot 0.8%, Forbes 0.8%, Healthline 0.8%, eBay 0.7%, Edmunds 0.7%, NerdWallet 0.6%, KBB 0.6%, Tripadvisor 0.6%, LinkedIn 0.6%, JustAnswer 0.6%; NYT, Best Buy, Pinterest, CNET, Consumer Reports, Medium, Trustpilot, wikiHow, Yelp ~0.3-0.5%
- Pages cited scale: YouTube 1.74M, Google 1.22M, Reddit 831K, Facebook 514K — citation breadth rewards massive content libraries
- Mid-authority sites can rank: JustAnswer, CarGurus, and RTINGS sit in the top 50 despite modest authority scores
- AI Overviews reach: 2B+ monthly users, 200+ countries (per Google)
- Data basis: every domain cited across 3M+ US queries, ranked by mention share (share of summed top-50 citations)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Applying ChatGPT-oriented tactics (editorial mentions) to AIOs, where video/community dominate
- Treating YouTube as a branding channel only — it is the #1 citation source by a wide margin
- Publishing social/community content that isn't publicly indexable
- Overlooking Google's self-citation (8.8% of citations go to its own properties)
- Ignoring retail/marketplace listings for product queries — marketplace pages are the cited sources

## Key principles
- AI Overviews favor experience-first platforms (video, forums, social) over publisher content
- Citation breadth (pages cited) rewards volume + consistency, not single flagship posts
- The AIO citation market is more concentrated than classic SEO: top 9 sources ≈ 81% of tracked citations
- Match the platform's source bias: video for AIOs, community threads for ChatGPT, institutional sources for health/finance everywhere
