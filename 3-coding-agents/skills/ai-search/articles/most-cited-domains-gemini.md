# The Most-Cited Websites in Gemini (Industry Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 3M+ US queries found Gemini has the single most concentrated citation economy of any assistant: Reddit alone captures 28.5% of top-source citations, followed by YouTube (14.6%) and Wikipedia (8.8%). Below the platform giants sits a long tail of niche product-review and vertical-research sites — a genuine opening for specialized publishers.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Mention share = each domain's slice of top-source citations. Gemini's source mix:
1. **UGC platforms (~43% of top-source citations)** — Reddit 28.5%, YouTube 14.6%
2. **Reference** — Wikipedia 8.8%
3. **Retail** — Walmart 2.8%, Target 1.6%, eBay 1.5%, Best Buy, Home Depot, Lowe's, Ulta
4. **Business media** — Forbes 2.7%, Business Insider
5. **Vertical research/review long tail** — Edmunds 1.9%, KBB 1.8%, Car and Driver, Cars.com, CarBuzz, CarParts, RepairPal (auto-heavy); OutdoorGearLab, RunRepeat, Reviewed.com, Tom's Guide, PCMag, RTINGS-style testing sites; niche directories (Niche.com)
6. **Health** — Healthline 1.8%, WebMD, Medical News Today, GoodRx, PubMed/PMC

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Make genuine, disclosed Reddit participation the #1 off-site priority for Gemini visibility — no other source is close
- [ ] Niche review publishers: invest in genuine product-testing content (measured results, methodologies) — Gemini rewards specialist review sites over generalist media
- [ ] Retail brands: structured product data on marketplaces and big-box listings (Walmart outranks most publishers)
- [ ] Automotive-adjacent brands note the anomaly: auto-research domains hold ~8 of the top 50 slots
- [ ] Local/service brands: home-services and review-aggregator domains (BBB, Trustpilot, Angi) all place — keep profiles complete
- [ ] Track monthly; niche sites jump 10+ positions regularly

## Data points & benchmarks
- Gemini app scale: company-reported 750M monthly active users (up from 650M a quarter earlier)
- Top shares: Reddit 28.5%, YouTube 14.6%, Wikipedia 8.8%, Walmart 2.8%, Forbes 2.7%
- The most concentrated #1 source of any assistant studied (Reddit ~28.5%)
- Long tail is accessible: specialist sites with modest-to-moderate authority hold top-50 share
- Reddit cited across ~388K distinct pages; YouTube ~188K

## Mistakes to avoid
- Spreading effort evenly across platforms — Gemini's winner-take-most structure rewards dominating Reddit + YouTube first
- Generalist content against specialist review sites for product queries
- Assuming only high-authority domains get cited; niche depth wins Gemini slots

## Key principles
- Gemini's distribution across Search, Android, Chrome, and Workspace makes its citations unusually consequential.
- Platform concentration: win the two big UGC platforms and you've addressed ~43% of the citation economy.
- The Gemini long tail favors expertise signals: testing methodology, measured results, category specialization.
