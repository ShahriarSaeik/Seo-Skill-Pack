# AI Overviews Cite AI-Generated Content More Than Human Writing (Ouroboros Effect)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
In one study, running ~500K AI Overview-cited URLs through an AI-content detector (detectors are imperfect, so treat the bands as approximate) found only 8.6% "pure human" and 3.6% "pure AI" — 87.8% are human+AI hybrids — versus a web baseline where 25.8% of new pages are pure human. AI Overviews skew toward AI-assisted sources (AI quietly citing AI), but AI percentage has zero effect (0.017 correlation) on citation order: hybrid content neither helps nor hurts placement.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Study protocol:
1. **Extract top-3 cited URLs** from 1M AI Overview SERPs (1.9M links; ~500K analyzable).
2. **Run each page through AI-content detection** — classify pure AI / pure human / mixed, with AI-share bands.
3. **Compare against the general web baseline** (900K new pages) to detect citation bias.
4. **Correlate AI-content percentage with citation position** to test for penalty or reward.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Stop asking "AI or human?" as a binary for AEO — 87.8% of cited content is hybrid
- [ ] Use AI for drafting/structure; citations didn't penalize AI share (human review and fact-checking still apply)
- [ ] Invest the saved effort in what detectors can't supply: original data, first-hand experience
- [ ] Monitor citation drift as the AI-share feedback loop compounds over time
- [ ] Verify facts in AI-assisted drafts — hallucinated details in cited sources propagate into answers

## Data points & benchmarks
- AIO-cited pages: pure AI 3.6% | pure human 8.6% | mixed 87.8%
- Mixed-page AI share: minimal (1–10%) 11.2% | moderate (11–40%) 44% | substantial (41–70%) 24.7% | dominant (71–99%) 7.9%
- Web baseline (new pages): pure human 25.8% — cited pages are ~3x less likely to be pure human
- AI % ↔ citation order correlation: 0.017 (effectively zero)
- Context: 74% of new webpages include AI content; 86.5% of top-ranking pages are at least partially AI

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming AI Overviews favor "pure human" content — the evidence points the other way
- Avoiding AI tools out of citation fear — AI share doesn't change citation position
- Reading this as an endorsement of raw AI output — pure AI rarely wins top spots anywhere

## Key principles
- The ouroboros: AI answers increasingly cite AI-assisted sources — a self-reinforcing content loop.
- Selection is neutral on AI share; differentiation now comes from information gain, not production method.
- The web's default content unit is now "human + AI hybrid."
