# "AI-Proof" Keywords: Which Queries Still Click Despite AI Overviews

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A small-scale analysis of one site's keywords found that ~7% of queries kept or grew clicks even with an AI Overview present — and they cluster in three patterns: free-tool/utility queries, keywords newly ranking on page one, and SERPs where the AI Overview sits below position #1. You can find your own AI-proof keywords by cross-referencing improved-CTR queries with AI Overview presence.

## Framework / Step-by-step
How to profile your own AI-proof keywords:
1. **Export from Search Console** — keywords with impressions ≥ 100 and CTR change ≥ 0% ("improved") since the AI Overview rollout inflection (e.g., a core update after which AIO presence rose sharply).
2. **Cross-reference with SERP data** — filter the list for keywords whose SERPs include an AI Overview.
3. **Keep the intersection** — those are your AI-proof keywords; the queries that click despite the answer box.
4. **Categorize the winners** — tag each by pattern (tool, new ranking, AIO position) to steer future targeting.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Build more free tools and utilities — summaries can't perform the user's job-to-be-done
- [ ] Keep investing in rank improvements: moving into the top 10 still lifts CTR in AI-era SERPs
- [ ] Track where the AI Overview sits; SERPs with AIOs below #1 leave click breathing room
- [ ] Re-run the improved-CTR export quarterly to refresh your AI-proof keyword list
- [ ] Prioritize AI-proof patterns when choosing new content/projects

## Data points & benchmarks
- 97 of 1,393 keywords (~7%) kept clicks while triggering AI Overviews
- Context: AIO presence cuts average position-1 CTR ~34.5% (earlier study; later -58%)
- ~8.5% of AI Overviews appear outside position #1 (down to #6)
- Free-tool pages can hold position AND earn an AIO citation — two click chances on one SERP

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming AI Overviews kill 100% of clicks — a measurable minority of queries still click
- Only tracking aggregate CTR decline; the segment-level view reveals opportunities
- Abandoning rank improvements because "AI takes the clicks anyway" — new rankings still pay

## Key principles
- Utility beats summaries: when the query is a task, users must click.
- Visibility stacking (organic ranking + AIO citation) multiplies click chances.
- In zero-click search, the goal is to be in the click-surviving minority.
