# How SERP Features Evolved in the AI Era: Featured Snippets Are Shrinking (1M SERPs)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Comparing 1M US desktop SERPs (January vs. June 2025; one study, and other studies show lower overall AI Overview prevalence): AI Overviews exploded from ~4% to ~27% of SERPs (+598%) while Featured Snippets collapsed from 15.4% to 5.5% (-64%, correlation with AIO growth: -0.9). The sleeper winner is sitelinks (+906%, now on 85% of SERPs via scroll-to sitelinks). AI Overviews are absorbing the jobs of older answer features.

## Framework / Step-by-step
How the analysis worked (replicable):
1. **Sample SERPs randomly** (1M US desktop) from a keyword database.
2. **Measure feature presence per feature** at two time points (Jan vs. June 2025).
3. **Compute relative visibility change** per feature.
4. **Correlate feature trends** against AI Overview growth to spot substitution patterns.
5. **Identify switch-over points** — the March core update marks the AIO/featured-snippet flip.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Don't make featured-snippet optimization a core strategy — the feature is shrinking (~5% of SERPs in this data) as AIOs absorb it; concise, well-structured answers serve both
- [ ] Re-optimize former snippet-target pages for AI Overview citation (definition-style, extractable answers)
- [ ] Structure pages with clear section anchors to benefit from scroll-to sitelinks (now on ~85% of SERPs)
- [ ] Track sitelink and AIO-sitelink presence for your brand queries — internal navigation now matters more
- [ ] Review tactics built around declining features (shopping packs, paid sitelinks, local teasers) against your own click data before cutting them
- [ ] Re-run feature-trend comparisons quarterly — AIOs may keep absorbing other features

## Data points & benchmarks
- Winners: sitelinks 8.4% → 85.0% (+906%) | AI Overviews 3.9% → 27.4% (+598%) | AIO sitelinks 3.9% → 25.0% (+541%) | knowledge cards +54% | questions 73.7% → 79.1% | discussions +4%
- Losers: featured snippets 15.4% → 5.5% (-64%) | shopping 3.1% → 1.0% (-68%) | paid -53% | paid sitelinks -55% | local teaser -23% | local pack -15% | knowledge panel -8% | organic shopping -7% | news -5%
- Featured-snippet decline correlates -0.91 with AI Overview growth; the March update is the switch-over point (AIOs +116% that month)
- Sitelinks expansion driven by scroll-to sitelinks on non-branded queries (previously brand-only internal sitelinks)

## Mistakes to avoid
- Building 2025+ roadmaps around featured snippets — the surface is shrinking
- Assuming correlation = causation for the snippet decline, but treating AIO overlap as the working hypothesis
- Ignoring sitelinks because they're "old" — they quietly became near-universal

## Key principles
- AI Overviews are a universal answer surface: flexible enough to replace niche features one by one.
- SERP features follow a substitution cycle — track shares, not just rankings.
- Zero-click features compound: each new answer surface removes more click inventory.
