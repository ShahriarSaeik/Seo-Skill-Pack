# Do Mentions on High-Traffic Pages Drive AI Mentions? (Visibility Trilogy)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Mentions on high-traffic pages correlate moderately with AI Overview visibility (ρ = 0.55) and weakly with Perplexity (0.35) and ChatGPT (0.20). Across the three "exposure" studies, the predictor strength ranking for AIOs is: mention-page links (0.70) > branded mention volume (0.65) > mention-page traffic (0.55) — and the chatbot stays near-zero on all three, which suggests a different, retrieval/partnership-driven source model (correlations only, not proof of cause).

## Framework / Step-by-step
The visibility trilogy (same top-50-per-platform dataset, one month):
1. **Define "being seen"** — total estimated monthly organic traffic to all pages mentioning the brand.
2. **Correlate web visibility with AI visibility** per platform.
3. **Compare against the sibling studies**: mention-page referring domains (link prominence) and raw mention counts.
4. **Rank the drivers per platform** to allocate PR effort.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] For AIO visibility, prioritize link-rich placements over pure traffic plays (0.70 vs 0.55)
- [ ] Use high-traffic placements (big publications) for Perplexity — its correlation is the relatively strongest among non-Google assistants
- [ ] Don't rely on big-site mentions alone for chatbot visibility (ρ = 0.20) — check which sources it actually cites
- [ ] Combine all three mention strategies (volume, link equity, traffic) for compounding AIO effect
- [ ] Re-check correlations as assistants evolve their retrieval systems

## Data points & benchmarks
- Mention-page traffic ↔ AI visibility: AIOs 0.55 | Perplexity 0.35 | ChatGPT 0.20
- Full driver ranking for AIOs: linked pages 0.70 > branded mentions 0.65 > traffic pages 0.55
- Chatbot driver correlations across all three studies: 0.12–0.20 (consistently negligible)
- Dataset: ~77M AI Overviews, ~1M prompts each for chatbot and answer engine, single month

## Mistakes to avoid
- Equating "traffic of the mentioning page" with authority — links predict better
- Using one exposure metric to model every platform
- Ignoring that strong brands naturally appear everywhere (correlation ≠ causation)

## Key principles
- Not all mentions are equal: where you're mentioned (link equity) beats how much traffic that page gets.
- Perplexity sits between Google's brand gravity and the chatbot's partnership logic.
- Measure the driver that matches the platform you're targeting.
