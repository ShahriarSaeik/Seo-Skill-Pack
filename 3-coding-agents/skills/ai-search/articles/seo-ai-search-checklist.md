# The Ultimate 82-Point Checklist for SEO & AI Visibility

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A combined SEO + AI-search playbook in 7 sections: Branding, Auditing, Content, Link building, Technical SEO, Local SEO, Reporting. Core thesis: AI visibility is built on SEO fundamentals plus new tasks (AI bot access, brand mention building, AI visibility measurement, fixing misinformation in AI answers).

## Framework / Step-by-step
**1. Branding (10 items):** consistent identity everywhere; profiles on alternative platforms (Reddit, TikTok, YouTube); Wikipedia page accuracy; own branded SERP real estate; awards; reviews (Google, Trustpilot, Reddit); EEAT signals (author bios, credentials); trust signals on Home/About; paid ads on competitive keywords; campaigns that spark branded searches.

**2. Auditing (9 items):** brand visibility audit (names/logos/NAP inconsistencies); AI search visibility baseline; link + mention audit (reclaim lost/broken); audit top pages visible in AI responses; content completeness audit; find & refresh decaying content (dropped rankings, fewer AI mentions, declining AI traffic, outdated data); competitor gap analysis (brand/content/link); verify what AI says about your brand is true; AI brand gap analysis vs competitors.

**3. Content (25 items):**
- Topical authority: publish original data; keep most-cited pages fresh; close topic gaps; build content hubs (pillar + clusters); find questions (keyword research data, Search Console, question-mining tools, PAA, support tickets, voice-of-customer); SEO topical map beyond keywords; adapt strategy for AI (pages that should be cited but aren't)
- Quality/credibility: author bios; expert quotes; tone per format; optimize for jobs-to-be-done (templates/calculators, not just answers); define jargon on first use; original multimedia
- Structure: emulate formats AI cites (step-by-step, comparison tables, checklists, FAQs); TL;DR at top; logical scannable structure; one idea per section, bottom line first; single H1 + logical H2/H3; bullets/numbered lists/HTML tables (not images); FAQ sections with original answers (information gain)
- Classic on-page: keyword in slug, title tag, meta description, internal links
- Distribution: repurpose winners into video/carousels/podcasts/infographics across LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit

**4. Link building (9 items):** links + mentions around the web; mentions near topical-relevant content; partner with writers already cited in AI responses (find them via manual prompts or a cited-pages view; disclose any free product or payment); guest posts on relevant, AI-visible publications (editorial value first, no link-scheme guest posting); shareable assets (tools, data studies); fix misinformation/inconsistent brand info on third-party sites; reclaim lost links/mentions; contribute authentically to forums; get mentions on the most-cited pages for your topic.

**5. Technical SEO (14 items):** scheduled site audit crawls; HTTPS; robots.txt allows the AI bots you want; XML sitemap submitted; clean architecture; fast loading; check your CDN/WAF isn't blocking LLM crawlers (defaults often block!); Core Web Vitals; structured data + semantic HTML; hreflang; image compression (WebP/AVIF with fallbacks) + alt text; server-side rendering for JS; mobile-first; redirect broken pages AND URLs hallucinated by AI.

**6. Local SEO (5 items):** Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and Apple Maps profiles; location pages; consistent NAP; regional citations; keep profiles active.

**7. Reporting & analysis (10 items):** web analytics for traffic split by channel (incl. AI); Search Console + Bing WMT; brand awareness dashboard; monitor top pages organic + AI; track top-20 rankings; track links/mentions growth; AI share of voice vs competitors; brand appearances in AI answers (from an AI visibility tracker or manual prompt logs); brand↔topic association strength; track hallucinations + inaccurate brand info.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Work through the 7 sections in order; copy it into a spreadsheet to track progress
- [ ] Priority quick wins: CDN/WAF AI-bot defaults, robots.txt, author bios, TL;DR sections
- [ ] Baseline AI visibility before making changes so impact is measurable
- [ ] Add "AI-cited pages" to refresh cycle; treat hallucinated URLs as 301 targets
- [ ] Add mention tracking alongside rank tracking in monthly reporting

## Data points & benchmarks
- 82 total checklist items across 7 categories
- Emphasis benchmarks: reviews build trust for users AND models; HTML tables > table images
- Some major CDNs set AI-crawler blocking ON by default — check account settings
- Track top 20 (not top 10) positions for meaningful movement signal

## Mistakes to avoid
- Treating AI optimization as separate from SEO ("you don't need another debate... you need to know what to do")
- Uploading tables as images (unreadable by search/AI)
- Applying heading tags for visual effect instead of hierarchy
- FAQ answers that add no information gain
- Ignoring misinformation about your brand in AI answers (it spreads)

## Key principles
- "Search is no longer a single channel. It's a multi-layered ecosystem."
- The fundamentals of SEO still matter — AI search layers on top of them
- Mentions near topically relevant content strengthen authority; irrelevant mentions dilute it
- Track hallucinations as a first-class metric — false info compounds if uncorrected
