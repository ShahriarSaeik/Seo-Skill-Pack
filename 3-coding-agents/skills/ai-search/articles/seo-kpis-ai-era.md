# SEO KPIs for the AI Era: What to Track (and What to Ignore)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Most SEO metrics are proxies that don't tie to business growth. Only two classic KPIs earn an unqualified yes (search visibility and organic conversions); the rest are context-dependent or vanity. AI search adds four new metrics — mentions, citations, AI share of voice, and AI referral traffic — tracked for influence, not clicks.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Track as primary KPIs:**
   - **Search visibility (share of voice):** arguably the non-conversion metric most closely tied to market share growth
   - **Conversions from organic traffic:** the indisputable KPI; use data-driven attribution and check conversion paths for assisted credit
2. **Track conditionally:**
   - **SEO ROI:** only when stakeholders require it; measure at page/category level where investment and return are attributable
   - **New backlinks:** only if you actively build links and log outreach outcomes
   - **Branded vs non-branded traffic:** yes, especially non-branded — it isolates demand captured from strangers; branded traffic reflects brand-wide marketing
   - **Indexed pages:** only for large/complex sites at risk of indexing lag
3. **Add AI search KPIs:** mentions (brand named in answers), citations (your pages linked as sources), AI share of voice (mentions vs competitors), AI referral traffic (actual visits) — plus volume-weighted mention impressions to know which mentions matter
4. **Demote to diagnostics (not KPIs):** keyword rankings (use visibility instead), organic traffic totals (unless ad-monetized), tool-generated health scores, third-party authority ratings, CTR (page-level only), Core Web Vitals (minor ranking factor), engagement metrics (segmented, secondary)

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Define 3-5 KPIs max; everything else is a diagnostic
- [ ] Set up conversion tracking with data-driven attribution before judging any channel
- [ ] Segment organic traffic into branded / non-branded monthly
- [ ] Add the four AI metrics to reporting; note AI's small traffic share but outsized influence
- [ ] Review conversion paths to see organic's assisting role in multi-touch journeys
- [ ] Report SEO ROI per content investment, not per website

## Data points & benchmarks
- In one study, the largest chatbot drove just 0.21% of traffic across ~76K sites vs ~40% from Google — but AI answers influence decisions without clicks, so measure mentions, not just visits
- Example of traffic vanity: a "shrug emoji" page pulling 65K monthly organic visits while contributing ~nothing to a B2B pipeline
- Share-of-voice and market share move together — the strategic case for visibility as the headline SEO KPI

## Mistakes to avoid
- Treating engagement metrics (bounce rate, time on page) as SEO KPIs — flawed tracking, bad methodology, only useful segmented per page and source
- Chasing aggregate CTR or health score improvements that don't map to revenue
- Crediting branded-traffic growth to SEO
- Ignoring AI metrics because traffic share is small — influence precedes clicks

## Key principles
- Strategy is choosing what NOT to track; KPIs determine actions.
- Proxy metrics (authority scores, health scores) need context before they mean anything.
- AI influence is real even when AI traffic is tiny: recommendations shape decisions days or weeks before any visit.
- Non-branded visibility is the truest measure of SEO earning power.
