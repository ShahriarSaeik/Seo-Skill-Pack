# Brand Gap Analysis: Find Out Why You're Invisible in AI Search

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Brand gap analysis measures the difference between your brand's potential visibility and actual presence across Google, AI results, and the web — across 6 dimensions (visibility, narrative, topic, format, web mentions, demand gaps). It goes beyond content gap analysis: it examines every factor shaping discoverability and reputation. 7-step process from entity definition to stakeholder action plan.

## Framework / Step-by-step
**6 gap dimensions:** Visibility (appears less than competitors), Narrative (how AI/media describe you vs. desired positioning), Topic (should-be-associated-but-aren't), Format (cited content types missing), Web mentions (external sources naming competitors not you), Demand (branded queries signaling uncaptured awareness).

**7 steps:**
1. **Define branded entities + connect to topics** — list legal/common/abbreviated names + sub-brands, products, features, metrics, people. Map desired associations via keyword research: find adjectives/modifiers people use with your category ("affordable", "eco-friendly", "24/7", "no win no fee") — these become your benchmark "desired state."
2. **Benchmark current visibility** — a backlink analysis tool (authority score, backlinks, organic keywords/traffic/value) + an AI visibility tracker (mentions, citations, impressions, SOV).
3. **Find branded/unbranded keyword gaps** — list keyword ideas containing your brand name from a keyword research tool or Search Console: you should rank #1 for all branded terms. Investigate non-#1s: competitor/review site outranking you? comparison queries? owned asset (YouTube) ranking acceptably? Then unbranded topic coverage per priority topic.
4. **Find AI search gaps** — filter tracked AI responses: queries containing your brand (what AI says about you), branded queries where the citations don't include your domain (AI cites OTHERS), and multi-brand queries. Topic searches excluding your brand → gaps: not mentioned but a competitor is; incorrect/outdated info; weak topic association; missing cited formats; mentioned but not cited. Close via on-site content or third-party outreach.
5. **Web mentions gaps** — review the web pages that mention your brand: who mentions you, sentiment, which brand elements appear, publication types, strong formats, co-occurring topics, mention↔performance correlation. Find authors/publications covering competitors but not you → prospecting list.
6. **Competitor benchmark** — repeat steps 2-5 for competitors: what features/audiences/topics are strongly connected to them but not you (e.g., Quizlet: flashcards, AI, teachers/students, spaced repetition). Quick wins: advertise features on those topics, create awareness, then capture the demand via search.
7. **Strategy + stakeholders** — Fix / Build / Influence actions, prioritized by traffic potential, authority impact, AI visibility impact. Write up a stakeholder report.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Build entity table (legal/common/abbrev + products, metrics, people, sub-brands)
- [ ] Extract desired-association keywords (modifiers/adjectives in your category)
- [ ] Baseline: authority score, backlinks, organic traffic/value + AI mentions/citations/SOV
- [ ] Audit branded keyword rankings; flag every non-position-1 you should own
- [ ] Filter branded queries by citations: where does AI cite others for YOUR brand?
- [ ] Run topic-only AI searches excluding brand; list missing topics/formats
- [ ] Audit web mentions (sentiment, formats, authors); build outreach prospect list
- [ ] Repeat analysis for top 2-3 competitors; note their exclusive associations
- [ ] Prioritize Fix/Build/Influence by traffic + authority + AI visibility
- [ ] Deliver the stakeholder report

## Data points & benchmarks
- 6 gap dimensions framework
- Entity depth example: [main brand] + products + features + metrics + key people + sub-brands — the more connected entities, the stronger the knowledge-graph footprint
- Ideal state: position #1 for all branded terms (exceptions: own YouTube/LinkedIn ranking above site is acceptable)
- Prioritization scoring: traffic, authority, AI visibility impact
- Mention spikes should be checked against traffic/ranking/AI-visibility lifts

## Mistakes to avoid
- Confusing brand gap analysis with content gap analysis — it's every discoverability/reputation factor, not just keywords/pages
- Assuming brand name uniformity — legal entity vs. common name vs. abbreviation have separate profiles
- Ignoring branded queries where AI cites competitor/third-party sites instead of you
- Fighting for #1 where your own YouTube/LinkedIn legitimately ranks
- Chasing competitor-associated topics without checking business relevance (align or differentiate deliberately)

## Key principles
- "Your brand presence shouldn't depend on luck, algorithms, or guesswork."
- Search engines and LLMs infer brand meaning from how it's described/discussed — connect entities to attributes people care about
- Being mentioned but not cited = AI learns about you from others; take back the narrative
- Closing gaps compounds visibility and trust across Google, AI, and the web
