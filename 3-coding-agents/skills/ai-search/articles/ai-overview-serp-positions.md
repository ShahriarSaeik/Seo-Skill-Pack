# AI Overviews Appear Below Position #1 in 8.64% of SERPs (Down to #6)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Across 10M SERPs with AI Overviews (1M per country, 10 countries, July 2025), 91.4% place the AIO at position #1 — but 8.64% appear lower, as far down as position #6. Japan and Spain show the most below-position-1 AIOs. Lower-placed AIOs appear to steal fewer clicks, making them a click-survival pocket worth tracking.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Sample SERPs with AIOs** (10M total; 1M per country across 10 countries).
2. **Record the AIO's SERP position** per query.
3. **Segment by country** to find localization differences.
4. **Cross-reference CTR behavior** — queries where the AIO sits below #1 retain more clicks for organic results.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Filter rank tracking for keywords where the AIO appears below position #1 — these retain more click volume
- [ ] Prioritize optimization for markets/countries with more lower-placed AIOs (e.g., Japan, Spain in this study)
- [ ] Monitor whether below-#1 placement grows — it may suggest quality scoring of generated answers
- [ ] Track your "AI-proof" segments: keywords where the AIO sits below #1 tend to retain more clicks (the penalty is smaller, not gone)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Position #1: 91.36% of AIO SERPs | outside #1: 8.64% | lowest observed: position #6
- Country variance: Japan and Spain have the highest share of non-position-1 AIOs
- Related: later click-loss study found the CTR penalty persists at lower AIO placements but in diminished form

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming the AIO always occupies the top slot when estimating traffic loss
- Modeling CTR declines with a single uniform penalty across all AIO keywords
- Ignoring SERP position of the AIO in rank-tracking setups

## Key principles
- AIO placement is variable — and possibly quality-scored by Google.
- Where the answer box demotes itself, organic results breathe.
- Small placement shifts can mean material click differences at scale.
