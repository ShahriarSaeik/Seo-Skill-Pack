# Generative Engine Optimization: Growth Strategies and Metrics For the AI Era

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
GEO (aka AEO/LLMO) = getting your brand noticed and accurately represented in AI-generated answers. AI search still leans on traditional search (RAG), so SEO fundamentals carry over. 6-step playbook: web-wide mentions, AI-preferred content types, high-performing homepage/product pages, AI-comprehensible structure, multi-platform presence, mention/citation gap analysis — measured with AI mentions/citations/traffic, not rankings.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Get mentioned across the web** — most brand mentions come from third-party sites (top AIO pages mentioning one brand didn't include its own site; Perplexity the only exception). Unlinked mentions count. Channels: "best of" lists, PR/media, reviews/case studies (disclose any paid or incentivized placement). Prioritize mention targets by AI citation count, then domain authority score, then branded search volume.
2. **Create AI-preferred content types** — audit which categories earn AI traffic in YOUR niche (your analytics' AI referral channel). Mix content types across user intentions; keep core content fresh.
3. **Perfect homepage + product pages** — ~80% of one brand's AI referral traffic goes to free tools/product pages/homepage. Make value obvious instantly, structured, factual, parseable.
4. **Structure for AI comprehension** — hierarchical headings, bullets, short factual statements, schema, speed, mobile, don't block AI bots, minimal JavaScript.
5. **Go multi-platform** — YouTube (2nd most-cited domain in AI Overviews; 3.7B visits) and Reddit (77% of product review searches; 3rd most-cited; 952M monthly visits; older figures). Find Reddit threads that rank on page one for your keywords (a keyword research tool or site:reddit.com searches work) and add genuinely useful answers, disclosing any affiliation and following each subreddit's rules; undisclosed promotion gets removed and hurts trust.
6. **Find mention + citation gaps** — filter to prompts where only competitors are mentioned and review the cited pages for mention gaps; filter by competitor domain for citation gaps; build superior content for those topics.

**Measure:** AI mentions, impressions (mentions × search volume), AI share of voice, citations by platform, AI-driven traffic (an AI channel in your web analytics).

## Actionable checklist

## Data points & benchmarks
- 16% of US searches showed AI Overviews (doubled since Mar 2025); ~97% of AI Overview keywords are informational
- AI Overview present → position #1 CTR dropped 34.5% (0.056 → 0.031 for informational; earlier study, later re-run: -58%)
- In one company's data, AI traffic converted ~23x better than traditional search; results vary by business
- One brand: cited 7,470 times across 2,309 pages with zero deliberate AI optimization
- Branded web mentions correlation with AIO visibility: 0.664; branded anchors 0.527; branded search volume 0.392
- AI traffic by title pattern: "best" 7.06%, "contact" 6.8%, how-to 6.35%, "products" 6.43%, "top" 5.5%, "vs" 4.88%
- Case-study AI traffic split: free tools 36.45%, product pages 23.12%, homepage 20.41%
- Case-study content engagement: data studies 207s time-on-page, how-to guides 184s, opinion 214s; product page bounce 0.24 (low), tool listicles 0.84 (high)
- ChatGPT reached 100M users in about two months (early 2023); because assistants rely on search for current facts, SEO still feeds citations
- Google representatives have said specialized "AI SEO" isn't necessary; fundamentals matter most

## Mistakes to avoid
- Treating GEO as a replacement strategy instead of an extension of SEO
- Chasing only rankings-era metrics — rankings won't show the GEO story
- Blocking AI crawlers or relying on JavaScript-rendered content
- Publishing one content type only — missing citation opportunities from other intent groups
- Worrying that AI search requires a total strategy overhaul (Google has said it doesn't)

## Key principles
- "Traditional SEO was competing for the best storefront on a busy street. GEO is making sure the local tour guide knows about your business."
- Every new type of search is built on existing forms of content
- Possible future (speculative): search engines stay important to AI via retrieval, AI Mode becomes more prominent, agents act (not just answer), spam detection matures
- Success = mentions + citations + share of voice, not just clicks
