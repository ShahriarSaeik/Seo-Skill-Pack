# AI SEO Statistics Compendium (2025 Benchmark Set)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A consolidated benchmark set of 90+ AI-search statistics from industry studies (mostly 2025 data; re-verify before use), grouped by theme: AI search behavior, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, citations, bots, content/rankings, traffic/conversions, and brand visibility. Use these numbers to set expectations, size opportunities, and back recommendations.

## Framework / Step-by-step
How to use this compendium:
1. **Diagnose** — compare your AI visibility metrics against the baselines below.
2. **Prioritize** — pick plays where the data shows leverage (freshness, mentions, brand signals).
3. **Report** — cite benchmarks when justifying strategy shifts to stakeholders.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Benchmark your AI traffic share against the ~0.1–0.3% baseline before forecasting
- [ ] Check that cited/relevant pages were updated within 30 days (freshness is a common pattern among cited pages, not a guarantee)
- [ ] Verify brand presence per platform — 26% of brands have zero AI Overview mentions
- [ ] Use category AIO rates to decide where informational content is most at risk

## Data points & benchmarks
### AI search behavior
- Google sends ~345x more traffic than the big three assistants combined
- 80% of AI-cited sources don't appear in Google at all; only ~12% match Google's top results
- AI platforms cite content 25.7% fresher than organic results; 76.4% of one assistant's top-cited pages were updated in the last 30 days
- 86% of top-mentioned sources are NOT shared across the three major assistants
- 28% of that assistant's most-cited pages have zero organic visibility

### AI Overviews
- 1.5B+ monthly users (Q1 2025); present on ~16% of US searches / ~21% of keywords; +116% growth in months
- Position-1 CTR down ~34.5% (earlier measure; later re-run: -58%)
- ~76% of citations pull from Google's top 10 (earlier study; later update: ~38%)
- 26% of brands have zero AIO mentions; top 50 brands take 28.9% of all citations; top-quartile web-mention brands get 10x more mentions than the next quartile
- 1.9x more common on non-branded keywords; over-index on informational, long, high-volume queries; under-index on branded/local/short
- Highest AIO categories: Science 43.6%, Health 43.0%, Pets 36.8%; lowest: Shopping 3.2%, Real Estate 5.8%
- 8.64% of AIOs appear below position #1 (down to #6); 71.7% of AIO results aren't monetized
- UGC tilt: Reddit +3.4%, Quora +2.2%, YouTube +2% more than expected; Reddit in 5.5% of AIOs

### ChatGPT & citations
- Only 6.8–10% overlap with Google's top 10; 83% of cited URLs aren't in Google at all
- #1 AI referrer: >80% of AI traffic to sites; AI traffic +85% since January; sends slightly more traffic than Reddit
- 67% of its top 1,000 citations are off-limits to marketers (platforms/reference sites)
- 2.38% of its cited URLs 404 (vs. 0.84% Google baseline); favors news outlets
- Cross-platform: Wikipedia cited most by the chatbot (16.3%) > Perplexity (12.5%) > AIOs (8.4%); YouTube strong for Perplexity (16.1%) and AIOs (9.5%); Reddit/Quora top-10 only in AIOs

### AI bots
- 21 major AI bots active (2x since Aug 2023); AI bots = 18.9% of requests (2nd behind search bots 34.6%)
- Most-blocked bot: 5.89% of all websites; one bot's block rate grew 32.7% YoY

### AI content & rankings
- 74% of new web content includes AI; only 26% is fully human — and pure-human pages are just 13.5% of top-20 results
- 86.5% of top-20 content is at least partially AI-generated; no correlation between AI % and rank
- Pure AI rarely reaches #1 organically; human content was 4% MORE likely to be hit by a Google update
- 91.4% of AIO-cited content is at least partially AI-generated; AI % doesn't affect citation order
- 87% of marketers use AI for content; $131 vs. $611 per article; +42–47% publishing volume; 97% review before publishing; 84% don't disclose

### AI traffic & conversions
- AI traffic up 9.7x YoY; still ~0.1% of referral traffic (~0.25% of a site's total; 0.3% for sub-10K-visit sites)
- Average search traffic -21% YoY; 63% of websites receive some AI traffic
- Case: AI = 0.5% of traffic but 12% of signups (23x conversion); AI users click links 75% less
- AI visitors: 4 pages/visit (1.2 fewer than search), +8s dwell, +5.4% bounce

### Brands & AI visibility
- Correlations with AIO appearance: branded web mentions 0.664 > branded anchors 0.527 > branded search volume 0.392 > domain authority score 0.326 > referring domains 0.295 > backlinks 0.218; ad traffic/cost ~0.21
- 45.7% of Google searches are branded; big-brand bias strongest on Google vs. assistants

## Mistakes to avoid
- Quoting stats without dates — this space moves fast; re-verify before stakeholder use
- Mixing platform-specific numbers (e.g., applying one assistant's overlap rate to all)
- Using traffic-share numbers to dismiss AI presence — conversion quality is the story

## Key principles
- Citations reward freshness, brand signals, and third-party presence — not raw rankings.
- Every platform has a distinct source diet; benchmark per assistant.
- Track the trend, not the snapshot: most of these numbers changed materially within a year.
