# Do Mentions on Highly Linked Pages Drive AI Mentions? Depends on the Assistant

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Being mentioned on highly linked pages (pages with many referring domains) correlates strongly with AI Overview mentions (Spearman 0.70), moderately with Perplexity (0.40), and barely at all with ChatGPT (0.12). Google's AI surfaces appear to track classic link-backed prominence; the chatbot's source selection looks driven more by partnerships and its own retrieval logic than page-level link equity (correlations from a one-month snapshot, not proof of cause).

## Framework / Step-by-step
Study design:
1. **Take the top 50 mentioned websites** per platform (one month of data: ~77M AI Overviews, ~1M chatbot prompts, ~1M answer-engine prompts).
2. **Identify where each brand is mentioned** on the web and measure the referring-domain counts of those mentioning pages.
3. **Compute Spearman correlations** between mention-page link prominence and AI mention visibility, per platform.
4. **Compare across assistants** to expose different selection philosophies.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] For AI Overview visibility: prioritize earning mentions on link-rich pages (established resources, reference pages)
- [ ] For chatbot visibility: don't over-invest in link-backed placements — its correlation is near zero; pursue diverse mention surfaces instead
- [ ] Segment AI visibility strategy per platform — one "authority" playbook won't fit all assistants
- [ ] Track which platforms reward your link-adjacent PR versus your broad web presence

## Data points & benchmarks
- Mention-on-highly-linked-pages ↔ AI mention correlation:
  - AI Overviews: ρ = 0.70 (strong)
  - Perplexity: ρ = 0.40 (moderate)
  - ChatGPT: ρ = 0.12 (very weak)
- Based on top-50 mentioned domains per system, single month snapshot

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming link-building logic transfers equally to every AI surface
- Reading correlation as causation — prominent brands naturally appear on linked pages
- Using a single platform's behavior to model all assistants

## Key principles
- Each assistant appears to have its own trust model: link-anchored (Google), hybrid (Perplexity), partnership/retrieval-driven (chatbot).
- Page-level link equity still matters — but only where the platform lets it matter.
- Diagnose per platform before deciding where PR and link effort goes.
