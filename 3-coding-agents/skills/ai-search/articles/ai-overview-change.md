# AI Overview Volatility: Answers Change Every ~2 Days (43K-Keyword Study)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
An industry study of 43,000 keywords (16+ AI Overview observations each, over a month; a point-in-time snapshot) found AIOs rewrite themselves constantly: 70% of consecutive observations differ, content persists only ~2.15 days, and 45.5% of citations swap out per refresh. But semantic meaning stays fixed (0.95 cosine similarity) — the answer's "opinion" never changes, only its wording, sources, and entities. Conclusion: win the TOPIC, not the prompt, and measure aggregated visibility, not one-off mentions.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Expect citation churn** — being cited today says little about tomorrow; ~1 cited URL changes every time a query's AIO regenerates. Build for repeated re-selection, not one-time inclusion.
2. **Become the theme authority** — AI Overviews rephrase a stable underlying consensus; the brands/sources consistently tied to the TOPIC stay in rotation regardless of wording changes. Map which themes AI associates with your brand (an AI visibility tracker's topic view).
3. **Measure in aggregates** — track AI share of voice and visibility across a volume of prompts over time; single-prompt tracking is noise (100% today, 0% tomorrow).
4. **Refresh cadence over cache-hunting** — popular queries are NOT cached (search volume ↔ change rate correlation ~0); every AIO regenerates, so freshness signals compete continuously.
5. **Target non-consensus topics** — for topics lacking strong consensus, a single page refresh can produce a different answer with different citations; volatility = opportunity for new entrants.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Switch KPI from "cited for query X" to "share of topic cluster citations over 30 days"
- [ ] Identify the 3-5 themes AI already ties to your brand; strengthen content + mentions there
- [ ] Keep key pages substantively fresh so re-selection keeps favoring you
- [ ] Set monitoring cadence at least weekly (AIOs persist ~2 days; monthly checks miss everything)
- [ ] Don't panic-respond to single-day citation losses — check the trend first
- [ ] For contested topics: publish differentiated takes to enter the rotation

## Data points & benchmarks
- 70% pointwise change rate (content differs between consecutive observations); average persistence 2.15 days; true rate likely higher (checks weren't continuous)
- Citation overlap between consecutive responses: 54.5% — 45.5% of cited URLs are new each refresh (~1 URL swap per re-run)
- Entities: appear in 37% of AIOs, averaging 3 per response; 54% entity consistency between refreshes (46% volatile — mirrors citation flux)
- Semantic stability: 0.95/1.0 cosine similarity between consecutive answers — meaning is stable even as text/sources churn
- No caching: search volume vs change rate Spearman correlation −0.014 (none); popular queries regenerate as much as niche ones
- Basis: 43K keywords × 16+ AIO observations over one month

## Mistakes to avoid
- Celebrating (or panicking over) a single AIO citation snapshot
- Reporting daily citation movements to stakeholders — the unit of truth is the 30-day aggregate
- Assuming high-volume keywords are "locked" by incumbents — no cache, constant regeneration
- Chasing exact phrasing of current AIO text — it will be reworded within days
- Treating AIO losses like rank drops: the answer's core stance rarely changes, only its sources

## Key principles
- AI Overviews are dynamic on the surface, stable underneath: "they never change their mind"
- Probabilistic regeneration means every render is a fresh selection — you must stay selectable
- Topic-level authority is the durable asset; prompt-level citations are rented
- Volatility is highest where consensus is weakest — new entrants win there first
