# Real Estate SEO: 6 Things You Can Do to Compete With Big Sites

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Small real estate businesses can't out-muscle Zillow/Realtor on broad keywords, but they win on: Google Business Profile (map pack ranks above organic), hyperlocal/long-tail keywords (neighborhoods, buildings, street addresses, buying scenarios), buyer-journey content, trust signals (E-E-A-T), fast mobile sites, and local backlinks. Niche focus is the weapon; volume sacrificed for intent.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Outrank big sites with an optimized GBP** — map pack sits above organic results; list each branch separately (Google ranks by distance); reviews are a top ranking factor (not all need to come from transactions — helpfulness earns them too); list ALL services; add team/client photos (trust, not rankings)
2. **Target realistic keywords even with low volume**:
   - *Local/hyperlocal*: neighborhood, street, building, landmark, ZIP, district, city-comparison keywords (in a keyword research tool: run your areas through phrase-match keyword ideas → filter for "real estate, house, condo, homes, properties" → keep keyword difficulty roughly under 30, lower for new sites)
   - *"Yard sign searches"*: optimize for property addresses — zero individual volume but high-converting aggregate traffic from drive-by searchers (only with real listing data and unique content per page)
   - *Diversification play*: corporate site targets main real estate terms, individual brokers target lifestyle terms → 2-3 page-one placements
   - *Building/condo landing pages*: templated pages for specific buildings can outrank Zillow for years when each has genuinely unique local content (not near-duplicate pages)
   - *Questions/terminology*: question keywords; term-explainer pages (example: ~795 monthly visits)
   - *Local guides*: "best, things, top-rated, event*, guide, list, tips, map, information, resource, transportation, park*, recreation, shopping" modifiers; mine "blogs about [area]" competitors' keywords
   - *Unique features/buying scenarios*: "pet-friendly apartments in [suburb]", "townhomes with low HOA fees" — low volume, high intent
3. **Create content for the buyer's journey** — informational guides (neighborhood overviews, school profiles) for early stage → transactional pages ("homes for sale in X") for late stage; every page delivers a logical next step: featured listings in guides, online scheduling integration, free valuation offers, always-visible contact bar
4. **Feel trustworthy instantly (E-E-A-T)** — TLS certificate; performance data + testimonials + recognitions; visible sold-properties portfolio; family/business story video
5. **Mobile-friendly + fast** — compress images, optimize code (image-heavy sites); mobile dominates real estate queries; check Core Web Vitals via PageSpeed Insights or a free site audit tool
6. **Backlinks from five sources**: directories (relevant niche directories, e.g., for historic homes), press (HARO-style journalist requests, expertise offers, listing features), podcasts/speaking (ask for links), local organizations/schools/events (sponsorships, career days, workshops, internships — tag paid sponsorship links rel="sponsored"/nofollow), terminology/data pages (become the citable source — find formats via competitors' most-linked pages)

## Data points & benchmarks
- Zillow: 4.5M+ keywords, ~32.7M monthly search visits in one older snapshot — and its #1 keyword is "zillow" (more popular than "houses for sale" or "apartments")
- Keyword difficulty recommendation for small players: roughly under 30; also look for top-10 results from sites with modest authority scores
- Example evergreen win: a building page outranking Zillow and Realtor for ~10 years
- Example terminology page: ~795 organic visits/month
- Real estate is YMYL — fact-check content, source data points with external links
- Most real estate keywords skew heavily mobile

## Mistakes to avoid
- Separate Business Profiles for offices that aren't real, staffed locations (suspension risk)
- Chasing broad high-volume keywords ("real estate", "investment property UK") — dominated by portals, wrong geography
- Assuming zero-volume keywords (addresses, features) have no value — aggregate traffic converts highly
- Pushing for immediate conversion from every visit — real estate journeys are long; guide the next step
- Skipping TLS/HTTPS — instant trust killer
- Unoptimized heavy images/virtual tours — slow site = lost rankings and visitors
- Only using stock photography — authentic realtor-taken photos win
- Building link tactics before learning good links from bad

## Key principles
- "Sometimes, being small is the biggest advantage" — niche down where portals won't go.
- The map pack (GBP) is the small guy's equalizer against big-brand authority.
- Reviews count for both rankings and buyer trust — helpfulness generates them, not just deals.
- Every page should move the visitor one step along their journey, not force a close.
- Become the citable local data source and links accumulate passively.
- Measure SEO by closed deals correlated to leads, not traffic alone.
