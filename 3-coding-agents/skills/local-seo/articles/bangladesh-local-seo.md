# Local SEO in Bangladesh: Practical Notes

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
The local SEO fundamentals (Google Business Profile, reviews, consistent NAP, a good website) work in Bangladesh too, with a few local twists: people search in English, Bangla and transliterated Bangla; addresses are informal, so the map pin matters more; Facebook pages and marketplace/delivery listings act as important citations; and reviews are easiest to collect through Messenger and WhatsApp.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Research keywords three ways.** For each service, check autocomplete for the English term, the Bangla term and the transliteration (e.g., "saree shop Dhanmondi", "শাড়ির দোকান ধানমন্ডি", "sharee dokan"). Use the most common form in your page titles and headings, and cover the others naturally in body text and FAQs.
2. **Fix the address and pin.** Write the address in one consistent format everywhere (House, Road, Block/Sector, Area, City, postcode), and drag the map pin to the exact entrance. Add a short landmark note in your website's contact section ("opposite X mosque") for customers; keep the GBP address field itself clean.
3. **Business name: match your signboard.** If your signboard is in Bangla and English, use the name customers know. Don't add keywords ("Best Biryani House Dhaka").
4. **Treat key platforms as citations.** Keep the same name, address, phone (+880 format) and hours on: your Facebook page, Instagram, food delivery apps for restaurants, online marketplaces for shops, and relevant local directories. Link back to your website where allowed.
5. **Collect reviews where customers already talk to you.** Send your direct Google review link via Messenger/WhatsApp/SMS after delivery or a visit. Ask everyone, not only happy customers, and reply to every review (Bangla or English, matching the reviewer).
6. **Make the website locally useful.** One page per service and per branch, with real photos, prices or price ranges, delivery areas, accepted payment methods (cash, cards, mobile wallets), and a click-to-call / WhatsApp button.
7. **Post updates on your Business Profile** for offers and events (e.g., Eid, Pohela Boishakh, winter collections), 4–8 weeks before seasonal demand.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Keyword list in English + Bangla + transliteration for each service
- [ ] One consistent address format; pin on the exact entrance
- [ ] Business name matches signboard, no keywords
- [ ] Same NAP and hours on Facebook, Instagram, delivery apps/marketplaces and directories
- [ ] Review link shared via Messenger/WhatsApp after every order or visit
- [ ] Replies to all reviews in the reviewer's language
- [ ] Service and branch pages with photos, delivery areas and payment methods
- [ ] Seasonal Business Profile posts planned before Eid, Pohela Boishakh and winter

## Data points & benchmarks
- No reliable public benchmark exists for Bangladesh local pack behaviour; test your own target searches from the target area and track them monthly.
- The same Google local ranking factors (relevance, distance, prominence) apply worldwide.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Relying only on a Facebook page with no Google Business Profile or website
- Different spellings of the business name or address across platforms
- Asking only for 5-star reviews, offering discounts for reviews, or buying reviews
- Keyword-stuffed business names to rank in other areas
- Targeting only English keywords when customers search in Bangla

## Key principles
- Global fundamentals, local habits: meet customers in the language and channels they use.
- Consistency beats volume: one accurate identity everywhere.
- Reviews are a habit, not a campaign.
