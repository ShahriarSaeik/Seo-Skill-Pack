# 9 Easy Local Link Building Tactics

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Links are one of the most prominent ranking factors in every major local/map-pack study, but local businesses rarely need many — first-page local competitors seldom exceed ~150 referring domains (one study). Work the nine basics: ranking-site directories, competitor link overlap, lost-link reclamation, local content, citations, community/sponsorship links, unlinked mentions, acquiring businesses, and internal links. Keep it clean: links bought or earned through value exchange (sponsorships, discounts, free products) should carry rel="sponsored" or nofollow.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Get links from other ranking sites ("Barnacle SEO")** — search your money terms, get listed on the aggregators/directories that rank (sign-up listings = consideration set + direct business)
2. **Get links your competitors have** — a link-intersect analysis (your domain vs. competitors) reveals niche directories and citation opportunities
3. **Reclaim lost links** — in a backlink tool, list your most-linked pages that now return 404 → 301-redirect dead pages to current equivalents
4. **Create locally relevant content** — "best grass for [area]", common local pests, local service pricing; attracts links via outreach more than naturally
5. **Get local citations** — US aggregators (Data Axle, Localeze, Foursquare) + majors (Google Business Profile, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, Yelp, Yellowpages, Dun & Bradstreet, TomTom) — US examples; use your country's equivalents
6. **Pursue other local link opportunities** — colleges (jobs, alumni pages; scholarships and student discounts are value exchange, so expect rel="sponsored"/nofollow and never offer them mainly for links), city directories, local news/magazines/podcasts, community groups (Reddit, Facebook, Nextdoor — be transparent about who you are, no covert promotion), sponsorships (sports leagues, races, meetups, charities; sponsor links should be tagged rel="sponsored"/nofollow), local awards; leverage relationships: supplier testimonials, case studies, ego-bait expert roundups
7. **Claim unlinked mentions** — best for news-covered niches (lawyers) or community-active businesses
8. **Acquire real businesses** — rare but valuable: buying a genuine competitor business can give you a stronger merged site. Only acquire operating businesses; buying expired or lapsing domains to pass ranking signals to your site violates spam policy
9. **Add internal links** — your own site is a local site you fully control

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Check the SERP overview in an SEO tool for target local queries — benchmark referring domains needed
- [ ] Search your top services; sign up/list on every ranking directory/aggregator
- [ ] Run a link-intersect analysis vs. 2-3 competitors; claim their directory/niche links
- [ ] Find 404s with links (most-linked pages returning 404, sorted by referring domains); 301 to live pages
- [ ] Publish local-relevant content; promote with email outreach
- [ ] Submit to aggregators (Data Axle, Localeze, Foursquare) and majors (GBP, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, Yelp, Yellowpages) — US examples; use local equivalents
- [ ] Pitch local colleges: job boards and alumni features; treat scholarship/discount pages as value exchange (rel="sponsored"/nofollow)
- [ ] Sponsor local teams/races/meetups/charities (tag sponsor links rel="sponsored"/nofollow); enter local awards
- [ ] Offer supplier testimonials and case studies for links
- [ ] Run an expert roundup featuring local figures (ego bait)
- [ ] Monitor brand mentions; request links on unlinked ones
- [ ] Watch for competitors selling their businesses (genuine acquisitions only; don't buy lapsed domains for their links)
- [ ] Build internal links from existing pages to service/location pages

## Data points & benchmarks
- Links are a top-ranking factor in every major local organic + map pack study/survey
- First-page local rankings rarely require more than **150 referring domains**
- Aggregators feed data to many other sites — prioritize them for citation reach

## Mistakes to avoid
- Skipping basics to chase trendy/creative tactics — riskier, rarely pays off
- Assuming you need huge link counts — local SERPs are won with modest referring-domain numbers
- Ignoring internal links because they're not "real" link building — you control them fully
- Letting 404s bleed accumulated link value

## Key principles
- Do the boring basics first; save creative tactics for when the business grows.
- Directories that rank for your money keywords are link targets AND customer acquisition channels.
- Local links come from being a good local citizen: sponsorships, jobs, awards, community involvement.
- Acquiring a genuine competitor business is the rarest but most powerful local link play; never buy domains just for their links.
