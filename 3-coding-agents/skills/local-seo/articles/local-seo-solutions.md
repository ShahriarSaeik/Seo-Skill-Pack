# Local SEO Solutions: 8 Tips to Boost Local Rankings

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A simplified 8-step local SEO checklist: optimize your Google Business Profile, do local keyword research, build searcher-focused landing pages, get citations + links, reclaim lost links, collect reviews, pitch "best of" listicles, and keep the site technically healthy. GBP + reviews drive the map pack; everything else drives organic rankings.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Claim and optimize your Google Business Profile** — top 5 optimizations only: right business type (storefront/service-area/hybrid), right category, accurate hours incl. holidays, photos, website link
2. **Do local keyword research** — brainstorm services → phrase-match keyword ideas in a keyword research tool → find the different ways people search (national volumes are fine for discovering phrasings)
3. **Optimize local landing pages** — dedicated pages per service; include what searchers care about (pricing, availability, types) — not keyword stuffing. Look at a top-ranking competitor page's top-10 keywords in an SEO tool or toolbar (ignoring sitelink/PAA noise) for "things to mention" (e.g., fixed price, emergency, same-day, gas/electric)
4. **Get local citations and links** — US aggregators (Data Axle, Localeze, Foursquare) feed other sites; majors (Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, Yelp, Yellowpages, D&B, TomTom) — US examples; use your country's equivalents; find industry/local dirs via a link-intersect analysis
5. **Reclaim lost links** — in a backlink tool, list your most-linked pages that now return 404 → 301 old URLs to new/relevant pages
6. **Get more reviews** — reviews are a top map pack ranking factor (#2 in a 2020 industry study); just ask (no incentives). Make reviewing easy: send before/after photos, share the review link from GBP
7. **Get featured on local list posts** — "best X in Y" lists bring customers + backlinks; find via Google with an SEO toolbar (look for posts with traffic), pitch the editor on merit; disclose any free visit/stay offered, and expect editors to tag such links rel="sponsored"/nofollow
8. **Keep your site healthy** — run a site audit crawl (free options exist): fix 4XX pages, missing titles, broken external links, orphan pages; track the overall health score over time

## Actionable checklist

## Data points & benchmarks
- Photos on a GBP → **42% more direction requests, 35% more website clicks** (older Google-reported figures)
- Reviews = **#2 map pack ranking factor** (2020 industry study)
- Citations = 5th most important local ranking factor (2018 industry survey, likely less today)
- Example finding: a boiler-repair page ranking for 232 keywords revealed searchers want fixed pricing, emergency callouts, same-day service, gas/electric specifics

## Mistakes to avoid
- Keyword stuffing landing pages — alienates customers, no SEO benefit
- Adding GBP website field as an afterthought — many owners skip it; it's basic
- Offering review incentives — violates Google's terms
- Focusing only on obvious keywords ("plumber london") — leaving long-tail service searches on the table
- Letting moved pages 404 — wasted backlinks; always redirect
- Ignoring listicles — they rank for "best ___ in ___" and customers use them to choose

## Key principles
- GBP + reviews win the map pack; keywords + on-page + links win organic — do both.
- Optimize landing pages for what searchers need to know (price, speed, availability), not keyword density.
- Aggregators multiply citations — list once, syndicate widely.
- Being on 9 of 10 "best of" lists = customers will try you.
- Make leaving a review frictionless and reviews write themselves.
