# Blog Post Ideas to Get Traffic: Proven Topic Patterns

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Random topics don't get traffic — topics with traffic potential do. Two repeatable methods produce unlimited ideas: keyword research (question keywords + difficulty, traffic-potential and site-authority filters) and competitor top-pages mining. The idea bank below shows the pattern types that reliably carry search demand across ten niches.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Keyword research for ideas** — enter a topic in a keyword tool → expand to related terms → filter for questions (can yield thousands of ideas) → filter: keyword difficulty under ~20 (lower for brand-new sites), traffic potential ≥ 500, and, if your tool supports it, keywords where sites with authority similar to yours already rank
2. **Learn from competitors** — pull a competitor's top pages (pages with most organic traffic); each is a validated topic. Example: one grooming brand's beard-styles post draws ~69,000 monthly visits from 9,542 keywords — a proven topic if you compete with them

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Pull question keywords for your niche monthly; save those with low difficulty (under ~20), traffic potential ≥ 500, and a realistic authority match
- [ ] Export 3–5 competitors' top pages; target their proven winners
- [ ] Map ideas to the pattern types above: how-to, best-of, "what is," "is X safe," itineraries, recipes, troubleshooting, ideas lists
- [ ] Prioritize by traffic potential, not personal interest alone

## Data points & benchmarks
- One validated competitor post: 69K monthly visits ranking for 9.5K keywords — single topics hide massive long-tail
- Head terms in finance/tech reach 100K–500K+; niche how-tos still deliver 350–10K

## Mistakes to avoid
- Brainstorming topics without checking demand
- Ignoring difficulty — targeting very high-difficulty topics (KD 70) on a low-authority site
- Duplicating a competitor's topic without a differentiated angle

## Key principles
- Master keyword + competitive research and you never run out of ideas
- Question queries are the densest idea source
- Every idea must pass the traffic-potential filter before it earns a slot on the calendar
