# Mid-Funnel Content: Your Secret SEO Weapon

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
AI Overviews are commoditizing top-of-funnel answers and Google's shopping UI is absorbing bottom-of-funnel clicks — but the mid-funnel (problem-aware → solution-aware searchers) resists both. Six underused MOFU content types — feature roundups, solution hijacking, quizzes, calculators, scorecards, and alternative assets — deliver higher SEO ROI because competitors ignore them and searchers want human guidance there.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Why the middle of the funnel:
- TOFU (informational) clicks are eaten by AI answers; BOFU (transactional) clicks are eaten by product tiles, filters, and in-SERP seller panels
- MOFU goals: move searchers from problem-aware to solution-aware, help them choose a solution, multiply brand touchpoints, build trust so you're first choice at purchase time
- Users often click MOFU results even when AI answers appear — generic answers don't satisfy decision-stage queries; hard to commoditize, especially in B2B

The six content types (with discovery keyword filters):
1. **Feature/product roundups** — compare your own products or features against each other (not brand vs. competitor). Filters: "ideas," "best," "vs," "and," "or," "types," "alternatives," "comparison." Example: a costume store's "group costume ideas" listicle where every item is a product they sell
2. **Solution hijacking** — target people already solution-aware but for the WRONG solution (e.g., spreadsheet templates for accounting-searchers, pitched with a free tool trial — ~6,400 monthly sessions for one brand at the time of the analysis). Be honest: the content must genuinely help readers, not bait and switch. Rule: avoid strong purchase intent (if the SERP is all shopping results and product pages — e.g., a mattress brand targeting "waterbed" — the play fails; you need a MIX of blog posts and product pages ranking)
3. **Quizzes** — recommend a solution from answers (MOFU = "perfect routine for your skin type," not just "what's your skin type" = TOFU). Filters: "quiz," "test," "what's my," "find your," "finder," "recommended." Top-ranking quiz pages are usually barely optimized — quick wins
4. **Niche calculators** — no-code interactive tools answering purchase-critical math. Filters: "calculate," "calculator," "ratio," "how much," "formula," "estimate." Example: a flooring calculator with ~100 words and 16 links pulls 8,500+ monthly visits; total traffic potential (what the top page earns across all its keywords) can be ~6x the head keyword's search volume
5. **Scorecards** — grade the searcher's current performance to surface problems (problem-awareness first; quizzes = solution-awareness). Filters: "rate my," "how good is my," "how bad is my," "checker," "grader" (you often can't filter for "scorecard" directly)
6. **Alternative assets** — downloadable working files: templates, CAD blocks, wiring diagrams. Filters: file extensions (.svg, .psd, .ai), "spreadsheet/diagram/blueprint," industry software names. Goldmine for narrow B2B verticals with few TOFU/BOFU searches — manufacturers often already have these assets internally

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Re-run keyword research with all six filter sets on your category terms
- [ ] Check intent mix before committing: blog posts + product pages in the SERP = MOFU opportunity; pure shopping results = skip
- [ ] Build quizzes/calculators with no-code tools; optimize the landing page text (most competitors haven't)
- [ ] Compare your own products/features against each other in roundups
- [ ] Package internal assets (templates, design files) the audience already needs
- [ ] Prioritize narrow-vertical B2B clients where TOFU/BOFU volume doesn't exist

## Data points & benchmarks
- One solution-hijacking program: ~6,400 monthly organic sessions from template pages
- Flooring calculator: ~100 words, 16 links, 8,500+ monthly visitors
- Example calculator keyword: low difficulty, mobile-heavy volume, traffic potential about 6x search volume
- Failed counter-example: commercial-intent "waterbed" pages for a brand that doesn't sell waterbeds → traffic decline, unrecoverable

## Mistakes to avoid
- Hijacking solutions whose keywords have clear purchase intent for products you don't sell
- Building quizzes/calculators without optimizing the landing page (rarely done by competitors — free edge)
- Ignoring MOFU because volumes look smaller — ROI per visit is higher
- Sticking to blog posts when the real asset format is a file, tool, or template

## Key principles
- MOFU is where searchers still prefer human/expert judgment over AI summaries
- Creativity is the moat: the discovery filters are the strategy's core skill
- The best MOFU asset moves a buyer exactly one step closer to purchase
