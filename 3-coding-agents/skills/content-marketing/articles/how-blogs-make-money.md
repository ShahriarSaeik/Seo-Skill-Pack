# How Do Blogs Make Money: 10 Ways to Cash In

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Only ~5–8% of bloggers earn a full-time income, but the playbook is well understood: ten monetization methods (ads, affiliate, sponsored posts, services, products, courses, coaching, speaking, premium communities, blog flipping), stacked into multiple income streams on top of search-driven traffic and an email list.

## Framework / Step-by-step
The 10 monetization methods, with realistic earnings:
1. **Advertising** — display/banner ads via direct deals or any ad network. typically about $5–$40 RPM ($0.005–$0.04 per pageview), more in premium niches. Premium networks require traffic minimums (often tens of thousands of monthly sessions; check current terms); self-serve networks require hands-on management below ~10,000 visits.
2. **Affiliate marketing** — commissions on referred purchases. Networks pay 1–10%; private programs pay up to 90%. A quality affiliate niche site can reach four figures/month within 12 months. Disclose affiliate relationships clearly and mark affiliate links rel="sponsored".
3. **Sponsored content** — brands pay for posts/features. $25–$1,000+ per post depending on traffic and influence. Label sponsored posts as such and mark paid links rel="sponsored".
4. **Selling services** — consulting, marketing services. $60–$300/hour for consulting. Hard to scale: clients want the expert, not staff.
5. **Selling physical/digital products** — ebooks, merchandise. ~$4 (book) to ~$50 (merch). Digital products scale best: build once, sell indefinitely.
6. **Online courses** — $400–$1,000 per sale typical.
7. **Coaching/mentoring** — $50–$150/hour.
8. **Public speaking** — $500–$10,000 per event.
9. **Premium communities** — exclusive content, masterminds, networking. $50–$150 per monthly membership.
10. **Blog flipping** — buy, improve, sell. Content sites sell at a 20–40x monthly profit multiple ($1,000/month profit ≈ $20K–$40K sale); average content site price was ~$101,400 (older figure).

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Choose a profitable niche first — high-ticket items are a must for affiliate monetization
- [ ] Stack 2–3 monetization methods; never rely on a single income stream
- [ ] Differentiate: personal experience, unboxing/review videos, custom visuals, or filling an unfilled gap
- [ ] Use SEO to write only about things people search for (compounding, unpaid-click traffic)
- [ ] Build an email list — one algorithm update can halve search traffic overnight
- [ ] Expand to adjacent channels (social, video, podcast) for extra monetization surface

## Data points & benchmarks
- Only 5–8% of bloggers earn a full-time income (older survey).
- 600+ million blogs among 1.9 billion websites; 6+ million posts published daily (older estimates).
- A single "best dog food" review page can pull thousands of monthly organic visits ranking for hundreds of keywords.
- One well-known blogger sold his site for $50 million after 12 years.
- Content site valuation multiple: 20–40x monthly profit.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Starting with display ads at low traffic — the math doesn't work and UX suffers.
- Choosing a niche with no high-ticket products for affiliate.
- Sponsored content that destroys your unbiased voice, or that isn't disclosed and tagged rel="sponsored".
- Subpar service delivery — it kills repeat clients and reputation.
- Writing about topics nobody searches for.

## Key principles
- Monetization strategy choice matters as much as content quality.
- Digital products and communities scale; time-based services don't.
- Traffic diversity (search + email + social) is income insurance.
- Competitive niches are fine — competitors give you a proven blueprint to out-execute.
