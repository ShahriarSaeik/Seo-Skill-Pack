# Repurposing Content: How to Get More Mileage From Your Existing Content

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Repurposing reuses existing content in new formats/channels to reach more people at lower cost. Embrace a zero-click mindset, repurpose only proven top performers, start with similar channels, and adapt (don't copy-paste) for each platform.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Embrace the 'zero-click' mindset** — Repurposing is distribution, not clickbait. Content is a vessel for an idea (your brand's core idea). Success = idea lodged in brains, not clicks. Multi-channel exposure builds top-of-mind preference for purchase time.
2. **Find your best-performing content** — Don't repurpose everything; prioritize winners. Blogs: GA4 or any web analytics (Engagement → Pages and screens). Videos: most-viewed on YouTube. Performance in one channel signals demand for the topic.
3. **Start with channels that are similar** — Blog → text platforms (LinkedIn, X). Repurposing is NOT easy/one-to-one (video needs storyboarding, recording, editing). Some well-known creators repurpose at scale only because they have dedicated teams. Confirm your audience is on the channel first.
4. **Adapt your content for each channel** — Same post performs differently on different platforms (one practitioner's test). Example video→post workflow: check search volume in a keyword research tool ("affiliate marketing": 175K/mo) → Identify intents in SERP overview (people want "what is" + "how to start") → content-gap report on the top 3 pages for required subtopics → reformat script into beginner's guide → ~8,800 monthly visits at the time of the analysis.

## Actionable checklist (9 repurposing plays)
- [ ] Video → blog post (use ChatGPT to convert script with target subheadings — treat output as outline, not final)
- [ ] Blog post → video
- [ ] Video series → course (validate demand: a keyword research tool filtered for "courses", "training")
- [ ] Individual videos → one long compilation video (one ~2-hour course compilation: 2.6M views)
- [ ] Blog posts → book/ebook (one brand turned its guide into a book)
- [ ] Long videos → short-form clips (use YouTube's built-in Shorts tools or any video editor) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts/Facebook
- [ ] Comprehensive pillar posts → guest posts via the "Splintering Technique" (each chapter = a guest article; find sites with a content research tool, one page per domain, excluding subdomains and homepages; keep it editorial and tag any paid placement rel="sponsored")
- [ ] Blog posts/videos → social posts (extract each tip, takeaway, custom image)
- [ ] Blog posts → Reddit posts (tl;dr version, strip links, single link at end; follow the subreddit's rules and disclose it's your content)

## Data points & benchmarks
- "Affiliate marketing": 175,000 monthly searches; the repurposed article got ~8,800 monthly Google visits at the time of the analysis
- Repurposed LinkedIn post (single article use case): >25,000 impressions
- Stitched course compilation video: 2.6M views
- Featured experts tend to share your content: one guide featuring 14 marketing leaders earned multiple shares from them

## Mistakes to avoid
- Expecting repurposing to drive clicks (it's distribution/brand-building)
- Repurposing non-performing content
- Posting identical content everywhere without adaptation
- Publishing a video script verbatim as a blog post without SEO optimization
- Promotional Reddit posts — must be genuinely helpful, links stripped except one at end
- Treating ChatGPT output as publishable — use it as an outline only

## Key principles
- "Just because you've published something doesn't mean everyone has seen it."
- Zero-click content: standalone value on each platform; the idea matters, not the format or the click.
- Repurposing saves money vs. creating net-new content but is not free.
- One popular creator masterclass pattern: one topic → podcast → newsletter/blog post → X thread → LinkedIn post.
