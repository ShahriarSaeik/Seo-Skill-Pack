# 53 AI Marketing Statistics for 2025

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A curated statistical snapshot (2025 data; figures vary by study and date, so re-check before citing) of how AI is reshaping marketing: AI Overviews appeared for roughly one in ten searches in these studies and cut clicks by ~34.5% in one; AI referral traffic is small but grew ~10x since 2024; off-site brand mentions correlate most strongly with AI visibility (more than backlinks or ads); and most ranking pages contain some AI-generated content, with no correlation between AI share and rank.

## Framework / Step-by-step
Group your AI monitoring into six lenses:
1. AI Overviews impact (SERP presence, click loss)
2. Search behavior inside AI answers (pages/visit, dwell, bounce)
3. AI referral traffic (volume, growth, what content attracts it)
4. Brand visibility in AI (mentions, correlation drivers)
5. AI use in content creation (adoption, editing, output)
6. AI bot behavior (crawlers, block rates)

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Check whether your priority keywords trigger AI Overviews (more likely: informational, long, high-volume queries)
- [ ] Track AI referral traffic as a channel, but expect it near ~0.1% of total referrals today
- [ ] Earn off-site brand mentions and branded search legitimately (no undisclosed promotion or fake reviews) — the strongest AI visibility correlates
- [ ] Publish best-of lists (honestly ranked, with any self-promotion disclosed), product pages, and in-depth guides (top AI traffic drivers)
- [ ] Keep human review on all AI-assisted content (industry norm: ~97% edit/review)
- [ ] Decide deliberately which AI crawlers to allow vs. block

## Data points & benchmarks
- AI Overviews: 1.5B monthly users; grew 116% after the March core update; show for ~9.5% of desktop keywords (16% US), 12.8%+ of searches by volume (prevalence differs widely by study and period); reduced clicks by 34.5% in one study; 43% of their links point back to Google itself
- AI Overviews skew to informational, longer, high-volume, non-monetized queries; skew away from branded/local/short queries
- AI-platform visitors: view 4 pages/visit (1.2 fewer than search visitors), spend ~8 seconds longer on site, bounce 4.1% more than search users; 7 in 10 never read past the first third of an AI Overview; Reddit/YouTube/forums absorb ~a third of leftover clicks; 90% of buyers in one survey say they click through to sources cited in AI Overviews
- Referral traffic: AI sends ~0.1% of total referral traffic; grown ~9.7x since 2024; Google still sends ~210x more traffic than the three main AI assistants combined; small sites (<10K visits) get up to 0.3% of traffic from LLMs vs 0.1% for 1M+ sites
- Brand visibility: top 50 domains hold 28.9% of AI mentions; only 14% of top sources overlap across the three major AI assistants; top-mention brands earn up to 10x more AI Overview mentions than the next quartile; 26% of brands have zero AI Overview mentions; ranking #1 in classic results gives ~25% chance of being an AI Overview source
- Content creation: 74.2% of new webpages contain AI-generated content; 86.5% of top-ranking pages contain some AI content; 91.4% of AI Overview-cited pages do; no correlation between AI content share and rank or citation order; 87% of marketers use AI to create content; AI users publish 42% more (median 17 vs 12 articles/month); 97% edit and review AI output, only ~4% publish raw AI content; blog posts are the most common AI-assisted format (87%); top tasks: brainstorming (76%), outlining (73%), updates (67%)
- AI bots: 21 major AI bots active (doubled since Aug 2023); the most-blocked bot is blocked by 5.89% of websites; another major bot's block rate grew 32.67% in a year

## Mistakes to avoid
- Assuming AI content share hurts rankings — the data shows no correlation
- Chasing AI referral traffic at the expense of search (210x gap)
- Treating all AI assistants as one channel — source overlap is only 14%
- Ignoring AI Overviews because traffic looks fine — click loss hits informational queries first

## Key principles
- Off-site brand signals (web mentions, brand anchors, branded search volume) beat backlinks and paid ads for AI visibility
- Best-of content, product pages, and guides earn the most AI traffic
- Different AI assistants prefer different sources (encyclopedias dominate; one favors news, another favors video and user-generated content)
- Human review remains the industry standard for AI-assisted publishing
