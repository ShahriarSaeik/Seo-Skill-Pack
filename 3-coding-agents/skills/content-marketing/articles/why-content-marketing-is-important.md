# Why Is Content Marketing Important? 7 Reasons to Start

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Seven reasons content marketing beats channel alternatives: it nurtures leads through long journeys, teaches customers to use (and therefore buy) your product, compounds and survives without constant spend, can be far cheaper than paid in the long run, fuels word of mouth, builds trust and authority — and your competitors are already doing it.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Nurtures and converts leads** — buyers move through ~10 awareness steps before purchase (problem-unaware → solution comparison); content is the bridge at each step (e.g., rank for "how to get more traffic," teach why your channel is the answer)
2. **Educates potential customers** — "people first learn how to use your tool, and sign up because they know how to use it." Teaching problem-solving with your product creates customers who get immediate value → better acquisition AND retention
3. **Scalable and long term** — evergreen search content keeps earning rankings and traffic even if the team pauses; paid stops the second spend stops
4. **Cheaper in the long term** — one large blog generating ~657,000 monthly search visits (older figure) would have cost an estimated $720K/month ($8.6M/year) to buy as ads; content costs a fraction
5. **Fuels word of mouth** — useful content gets shared unprompted by practitioners to their own audiences
6. **Builds trust** — solving problems repeatedly makes you the audience's authority; they return with questions and buy what you recommend
7. **Competitors are doing it** — in one older survey, 71% of marketers said content became MORE important to their org that year and 82% actively invested. If you abstain, competitors own the search results, the trust, and the nurturing for every Googled problem your buyers have

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Map your buyer's 10-step journey; identify which steps have no content
- [ ] Create teach-with-the-product content so users arrive pre-trained
- [ ] Prioritize evergreen, search-demand topics for compounding traffic
- [ ] Calculate your blog's ad-equivalent traffic value to make the budget case
- [ ] Make content good enough to be shared without asking

## Data points & benchmarks
- ~657K monthly visits ≈ $720K/month in equivalent ad spend ($8.6M/year) (older figure; ad-equivalent value is an estimate)
- Older survey: 71% of marketers said content marketing's importance increased in the past year; 82% actively invested

## Mistakes to avoid
- Expecting instant results — content compounds, it doesn't spike (that's paid's job)
- Judging content only by direct conversions; most value is upstream of the sale
- Ceding branded and problem-solving SERPs to competitors

## Key principles
- Education precedes purchase: teach first, sell second
- Content-driven organic traffic keeps arriving after budget cuts; paid traffic stops when spend stops
- Trust is accumulated one solved problem at a time
