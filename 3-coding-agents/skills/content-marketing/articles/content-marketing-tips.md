# 17 Actionable Content Marketing Tips

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
A quick-reference list spanning the whole content workflow: pick searchable topics with business value, match intent, systemize with a calendar, promote and repurpose, keep content fresh and opinionated, mix content types, use AI for menial work, publish original research, and add video.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Focus on SEO** — keyword research on an industry topic yields thousands of candidate keywords; consistent traffic comes from ranking for searched topics
2. **Know your audience** — writing for the wrong people yields visitors who never convert (e.g., a CRO agency attracting other CRO pros, not leads); define the audience in writing and share it with the team
3. **Swipe competitors' best topics** — their top pages by search traffic are pre-validated topic lists
4. **Prioritize business value** — score 0–3 how naturally the product fits the topic; publish only topics scoring 2 or 3
5. **Optimize for search intent** — study the SERP format ("best headphones" = fresh, up-to-date lists) and mirror it
6. **Create a content calendar** — topic, author, contributors, status, due date; this discipline powers 2+ posts/week for years
7. **Design shareable images** — visuals that simplify complex topics get shared (one checklist illustration: 1,000+ likes, 300+ reposts; hand-drawn stick figures can reach 1.6M views — production value is optional)
8. **Promote across channels** — newsletter features, authors sharing on social, outreach to people mentioned, paid amplification; new sites need even more
9. **Repurpose top performers** — guide → video, post → thread; content shouldn't end at publish
10. **Don't sweat word count** — one study of 900M pages (correlation, not causation): the average 10,000-word post gets LESS traffic than the average 2,000-word post; cover the depth needed and stop
11. **Keep content updated** — one refresh of a decaying tools list shot traffic up; audit for outdated info and intent shifts
12. **Be opinionated** — a CMO's rant about bad outreach earned 2,200 backlinks from 469 sites and is still cited years later; in the AI era, opinion is the differentiator
13. **Publish different content types** — no single piece hits every goal: search content (traffic), linkbait (links), sales enablement (deals), thought leadership (authority), free tools (leads), product updates (retention)
14. **Build content hubs** — interlinked collections around one topic; organize your best content for discovery + SEO
15. **Use AI as a content partner** — delegate headlines, ideas, outline expansion, rewording, quick conclusions; keep humans on strategy, SME interviews, experience, and opinions
16. **Publish original research** — journalists need data to cite; the most-linked pages on one large SEO blog are studies. Options: polls, surveys, partnerships, or your own methodology over public data (one indie ranking post: 322 backlinks from 201 domains)
17. **Reach more people with video** — YouTube is a major search destination (often called the #2 search engine); one brand's channel, running since 2018, reports 21M+ lifetime views and 450K+ subscribers and drives customers

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Run competitor top-pages monthly; keep a swipe file
- [ ] Write the audience definition down; circulate it
- [ ] Add business-value score to every topic in the pipeline
- [ ] Maintain a calendar with owner + status + due date
- [ ] Create one simplifying illustration per major post
- [ ] Plan promotion BEFORE publishing (newsletter, social, mentioned-people outreach)
- [ ] Refresh anything with declining traffic or stale facts
- [ ] Take a stand in every tentpole piece
- [ ] Map your content mix against the 6 content types
- [ ] Start one original research project and one video series

## Data points & benchmarks
- One 900M-page study: average 10K-word post < average 2K-word post traffic (correlation)
- Opinionated rant: 2,200 backlinks / 469 linking sites
- Indie research post (public data + own methodology): 322 backlinks / 201 domains
- Content cadence benchmark: 2+ posts/week sustained for years via calendar

## Mistakes to avoid
- Writing for peers instead of buyers
- Padding content to hit word counts
- Publishing without promotion
- Expecting one content type to serve every goal

## Key principles
- Steal proven topics, add your own angle
- Opinions and original data are link magnets
- Refreshing old content is cheaper than creating new
