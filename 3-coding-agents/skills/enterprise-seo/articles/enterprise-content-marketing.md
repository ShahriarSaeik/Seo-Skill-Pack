# Enterprise Content Marketing Strategy To Drive Growth

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Even with domain authority, enterprises must deliberately choose content and put in the work. Start with competitor-proven topics, build a base team (SEO + writer + editor), expand from BOFU to TOFU, and scale through translation, free tools, programmatic pages (only with unique data, never thin templated pages), and video. Progress beats perfection: bootstrap, show wins, climb the maturity model toward centralization.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Pick what to create**: start with competitor top pages (export + combine) or a content-gap export clustered by parent topic. Sequence: BOFU transactional first → informational + video → events/courses/ebooks/white papers/podcasts as resources grow. Don't skip TOFU forever — it widens the pipeline and owns expert positioning.
2. **Produce by creator type**:
   - SEOs writing: interview internal experts; repurpose their papers/talks; mine sales team insights; add to existing knowledge (no copycat content)
   - Writers: run card-sorting exercises (search data + top-page topics on index cards → writers group into outlines); or hand them content briefs; track performance per author (group their URLs in your reporting tool)
   - Experts: empower them to write, or interview them / have them review drafts
3. **Improve existing content**: declining-traffic pages (top pages with traffic declining over 6–12 months), low-hanging fruit (positions 4–15), featured snippet gaps (#1–5 rankings where target doesn't own the snippet).
4. **Scale plays**: translate winners, syndicate, redirect 404s with links, internal links, "vs" pages (avoid feature tables; use user proof + data quality), free tools, programmatic pages from proprietary data (each page needs unique value, or it's scaled content abuse), video structured around search queries.
5. **Build the team**: bootstrap with SEO + writer + editor; add strategists/PMs/designers later; external contractors are often easier than headcount.
6. **Get buy-in and scale**: show revenue (SQLs/MQLs) broken down by author/product/BU; join training initiatives; build BU scorecards; integrate with paid (retargeting) and social (amplification); align with "top 20/top 50" corporate growth projects; climb toward a Center of Excellence.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Export 3–5 competitors' top pages; combine into a seed content list
- [ ] Cluster content-gap exports by parent topic (a keyword research tool or your own grouping)
- [ ] Check search intent per keyword to reject intent-mismatched requests (keyword-sprinkling on product pages)
- [ ] Filter top pages for traffic declines over 6–12 months; prioritize refreshes
- [ ] Snippet hunt: keywords where you rank #1–5 but don't own the featured snippet → add concise, direct answers
- [ ] Run one card-sorting workshop with writers per major topic
- [ ] Translate proven content into other company languages; implement hreflang
- [ ] Reclaim links: top pages by links → 404 filter → redirects
- [ ] Fix generic internal anchors ("learn more") using a crawler's anchor-text export
- [ ] Launch one free tool and one programmatic template from proprietary data (unique data per page)
- [ ] Publish on an easy platform (blog) — avoid pages that need custom dev work per post
- [ ] Report wins as revenue by author/product/BU; repeat the "dog and pony show"

## Data points & benchmarks
- One large SaaS blog: ~8.2M organic visits/month ≈ $5.3M+/month in equivalent ad spend (a tool's estimate, older figure)
- Search-driven YouTube case study: 500k+ subscribers from <300 videos; many videos >1M views (one channel, not typical)
- Featured snippet trick: search "term -site.com" to reveal the next-eligible snippet
- Base team = 1 SEO + 1 writer + 1 editor

## Mistakes to avoid
- Copycat content — not future-proof; add new information instead
- Skipping TOFU — narrows pipeline, cedes "expert" status to competitors
- Optimizing product pages for informational intent — intent mismatch
- Requiring custom design/dev for every page — publishing friction kills output
- Waiting for perfect team/funding before starting — bootstrap and show progress

## Key principles
- "Big enterprise websites rank for everything" is a myth — they still have to choose topics and do the work
- Don't let perfect be the enemy of good
- Build momentum one win at a time; replicate success across BUs
- Align content pushes with the company's top 20/top 50 growth priorities
