# How to Rank for a Keyword (8 Steps)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Ranking for a specific keyword = validate the keyword (right topic + business value), reverse-engineer intent and subtopics from the SERP, add a unique angle (data/experience/effort), publish well-crafted content with basic on-page SEO, then build links. AI Overviews don't change this much — they mostly cite sources that already rank well in classic search.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Target the right keyword** — Check a keyword research tool: if traffic potential (TP: the traffic the #1 page gets across ALL its keywords) far exceeds the keyword's own volume, it's a subtopic — target the parent topic instead. Example: "how to create content" (500 searches/mo) → parent "content creation" (TP 1,200). One well-targeted guide can rank for ~2,100 keyword variations of its main topic. Rarely target zero-volume keywords (check TP first).
2. **Check business potential** — Score 1–3 how hard it is to achieve the task without your product (3 = tool required, e.g. "keyword research"; 2 = tool helps a lot, e.g. "content creation"). Prioritize keywords scoring ≥2.
3. **Figure out search intent** — Look at top-ranking pages (many keyword tools label the dominant intent). E.g. "content creation" = beginner's guides → you must publish a beginner's guide.
4. **Identify important subtopics** — Use content-optimization suggestions or study top pages. For "content creation": what it is, AI's role, creation steps, analytics, SEO's role, strategy, content types, tools. Google's helpful-content guidance (now part of core ranking) asks whether content is "substantial, complete, comprehensive."
5. **Develop a unique angle** — 3 E's: Experimentation (original data: experiments, polls, user-base data), Experience (firsthand insights, not theory), Effort (add what competitors would deem "too much work," e.g. testing every AI image generator instead of listing them).
6. **Create the content** — Grabby intros (short/bold claim/surprising stat); conversational human voice ("I"/"you," opinions); break up text (images, charts, bullets, one-line paragraphs); make advice actionable (screenshots, templates, exact steps).
7. **On-page SEO basics** — Keyword in title, URL, H1, intro paragraph; engaging meta description; internal links to useful pages; alt text on all images.
8. **Build links** — Every strategy = giving a reason to link (guest posts = free content; podcasts = free insights). Earn links editorially; paid or value-exchange links need rel="sponsored" or nofollow. Unique angles (data/stories/design) make link acquisition easier.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Look up keyword in a keyword research tool; switch to the parent topic if TP >> own volume
- [ ] Score business potential; skip anything below 2
- [ ] Study top-10 pages to fix content type/format (or use intent labels in your keyword tool)
- [ ] List all subtopics top pages suggest; cover them all
- [ ] Choose an angle: original data, firsthand experience, or visible extra effort
- [ ] Write with hooks, human voice, visual breaks, zero-guesswork instructions
- [ ] Place keyword in title/URL/H1/intro; write meta description; add internal links + alt text
- [ ] Plan link acquisition around your unique angle
- [ ] Crawl your site with an SEO audit tool to catch technical issues silently hurting rankings

## Data points & benchmarks
- "how to create content": 500 searches/mo vs traffic potential 1,200 for parent "content creation."
- A single well-targeted guide can rank for ~2,100 keyword variations of its main topic.
- Business potential scale: 3 = product essentially required; 2 = product materially helps; prioritize ≥2.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Targeting subtopics/phrasings instead of the main topic.
- Chasing keywords with no search demand or no conversion path (business potential < 2).
- Publishing generic, "good enough" content — increasingly invisible between AI Overviews and AI search.
- Ignoring intent: writing the "right" content in the wrong format.
- Skipping technical SEO — perfect content still fails if crawlers can't access/understand the site.

## Key principles
- "In the content game, 'good enough' is no longer good enough."
- Unique angle formula: Experimentation, Experience, Effort.
- LLMO/GEO/AEO ≈ SEO for now: AI Overviews largely source their citations from pages that rank in search — the fundamentals stay the same.
