# Topical Authority: What It Is, How Google May Assess It, and How to Build It

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Topical authority is being recognized as the expert source on a full subject, not just individual keywords. It's built by covering a topic comprehensively in connected clusters, staying narrowly focused (off-topic content actively dilutes your authority), and earning relevant links/mentions. It can let small specialist sites outrank giants and increasingly helps with AI search visibility. (It's an industry concept, not an official Google metric.)

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Topic-based keyword research** — pick seed keywords specific enough to focus but broad enough for subtopics (e.g., "coffee roasting," not "coffee"). Expand via related keyword ideas; find competitor topic gaps (keywords they rank for that you don't); check which subtopics AI platforms associate with your brand vs. competitors.
2. **Map topic clusters and pillar pages** — group keywords by intent into clusters; assign each a pillar page (guide, "what is X," "how to X") as the hub, with cluster pages as spokes. Combine thin subtopics into single pages rather than publishing shallow ones. Filter every planned subtopic: meaningful demand? business-relevant? within topical focus?
3. **Be selective — exclude off-topic content** — Google's leaked API documentation (2024) lists attributes called "site focus score" and "site radius," which suggest topical concentration matters, though Google hasn't confirmed how (or whether) they're used in ranking. Off-topic publishing is widely believed to dilute authority. Heuristic: "Would a first-time visitor immediately understand what this site is about?" If not, drop the topic.
4. **Write authority content** — definitive resources built on firsthand experience, original insight, and real expertise. Match intent per page; cover subtopics thoroughly; demonstrate E-E-A-T; internally link cluster pages together. Never fill clusters with thin AI/programmatic content — site-wide quality signals can suppress the whole domain.
5. **Build relevant links** — external: prioritize topically relevant links (a coffee niche site linking to your coffee blog) over high-authority but irrelevant ones; tactics include genuine guest contributions, linkable assets, ego bait, journalist quote services, digital PR/podcasts (never pay for untagged links). Internal: pillar pages link to all cluster pages and receive links back; cross-link related cluster pages; fix orphaned pages first.
6. **Keep clusters fresh and complete** — refresh on triggers (traffic drop, superseded stats, changed tools, competitor updates, lost AI Overview citation); run quarterly content gap analyses; cover emerging subtopics — AI query fan-out means more subtopic coverage = more AI visibility.
7. **Track over time** — branded topic searches (e.g., "[brand] keyword research" — variety matters as much as volume), unbranded cluster rankings/share of voice, and AI visibility on core topics.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Choose 1–3 core topics; build a full topical map (pillar + clusters)
- [ ] Run competitor keyword/content gap analyses per topic
- [ ] Reject any planned content that strays from the topical core
- [ ] Publish pillar page per cluster; deep, experience-based cluster pages beneath
- [ ] Hub-and-spoke internal linking; fix orphans first
- [ ] Pursue niche-relevant links and mentions; skip irrelevant high-authority links
- [ ] Quarterly: refresh decaying pages, close new subtopic gaps
- [ ] Track branded-topic query growth and AI topic visibility

## Data points & benchmarks
- One low-authority (~15 on a 0–100 scale) specialist e-bike retailer outranked Amazon (~96) for competitive e-bike keywords and wins AI Overview citations — topical depth beats domain authority in a niche.
- One authoritative health page appeared for 2,500 Google keywords, 473 AI Overview queries, 279 ChatGPT prompts, 200 Perplexity prompts — authority compounds across surfaces.
- Timeline: 6–12 months for significant movement (months 1–3 coverage, 4–6 early rankings, 6–12 compounding gains).
- Scale: a tight niche may need 15–20 well-connected pieces; broader subjects 50+.
- Google timeline: Hummingbird (2013, intent) → RankBrain → Medic (2018, E-A-T, now E-E-A-T) → BERT → MUM → Helpful Content (2022, site-wide signals; folded into core ranking in 2024) → AI Overviews (2023) → 2024 API leak (site focus score + site radius) → AI Mode fan-out (2025–26).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Chasing every keyword with volume regardless of topical fit — dilutes the focus signal.
- Publishing thin/AI-scaled content to "complete" clusters — site-wide quality suppression.
- Treating authority as a one-time build — gaps and staleness erode it.
- Confusing domain authority (backlinks) with topical authority (subject depth).

## Key principles
- Google shifted from ranking pages with the right keywords to featuring sites that own the right topics.
- Authority is niche-specific and exclusionary: what you DON'T publish defines it.
- Comprehensive subtopic coverage wins both rankings and AI fan-out citations.
