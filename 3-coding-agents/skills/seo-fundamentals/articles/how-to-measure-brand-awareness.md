# How to Measure Brand Awareness

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Brand awareness is the fuzziest growth channel — but measurable via 11 proxy metrics, from share of voice to branded search volume to AI mentions. Measuring matters more than ever: awareness compounds into AI citations, organic performance, channel resilience, and sales pulled along in the slipstream.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Social share of voice** — your brand mentions ÷ total market mentions × 100. The top-down read on whether all your activity is moving the market.
2. **Search share of voice** — your organic traffic ÷ total market organic traffic for your tracked keyword set. Track the trend monthly and benchmark vs. named competitors.
3. **Ad traffic uplift** — impressions, clicks, CTR before/during/after campaigns from ad managers; paid traffic growth vs. ad cost; campaign-specific filtering by keyword in ad copy. If paid traffic grows faster than paid spend (falling cost per visit), the awareness ads are likely working.
4. **Total & direct traffic growth** — direct visits are recall in action; align date ranges with campaign windows; segment by UTMs.
5. **Quality backlinks & referral traffic** — count only links that are relevant, authoritative, visible (traffic), and equity-passing. Track branded anchor text (brand name in anchor or surrounding text) and brand anchors as a % of total anchors.
6. **Social growth** — mentions, reach, and sentiment scores from listening tools; set queries for brand, products, campaigns, people.
7. **AI overview & LLM growth** — the new SERPs: number of keywords where your brand is cited in AI Overviews (tracked monthly), brand mentions across AI assistants, AI share of voice vs. competitors, topic ownership (entity associations), and LLM referral traffic by source in analytics. Watch which content types earn citations and make more of them.
8. **Branded keyword volume growth** — 45.7% of all Google searches are branded; track your branded keyword count, global volume, 3/6/12-month growth, and 12-month forecasts. Use for backcasting goals and modeling on the fastest-growing competitor.
9. **Branded search traffic** — how many brand searches reach your site: branded vs. non-branded traffic share over time; page-level brand traffic trend.
10. **Brand mentions growth** — unlinked mentions count monthly (web-wide content search + alerts); journalists rarely link, and in AI-era visibility, mentions function like links.
11. **Conversions & sales uplift** — attribution-window conversions during campaigns; awareness campaigns do convert sometimes — track it.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Stand up a monthly brand dashboard: social SoV, search SoV, branded volume + forecast
- [ ] Track branded anchors as % of total anchors; count quality-only new links
- [ ] Add AI metrics: AI overview keywords owned, AI share of voice, AI referral traffic by page type
- [ ] Measure co-mentions: brand AND key topic as % of all brand mentions (entity alignment)
- [ ] Correlate campaign dates with direct traffic and brand search spikes
- [ ] Report conversions during awareness windows, not just impressions

## Data points & benchmarks
- B2B companies spent ~29% of marketing budgets on awareness in 2024 — more than any other journey stage.
- 45.7% of all searches are branded (one industry study); one analysis suggested sites can lose a large share of clicks on FAQ/definition queries to AI answers while cited brands gain.
- Example benchmark: 23% of one brand's total anchors are branded (22K of 96K) — a trackable awareness ratio.
- Search SoV example: +6.2 points in six months to 23.1% total share on tracked keywords.
- One study suggests links show the weakest correlation with AI visibility of common SEO factors — mentions matter more.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Reporting raw link counts instead of quality-filtered links.
- Skipping measurement because attribution is imperfect — proxies beat nothing.
- Ignoring dark social and offline awareness you can't see (acknowledge the blind spot).
- Judging campaigns mid-flight; awareness compounds on lag.
- Chasing impressions without conversions — universal awareness without buyers bankrupts companies.

## Key principles
- Awareness is fuzzy; its proxies (SoV, brand search, mentions, AI share) are concrete.
- Brand strength is widely believed to help search performance (Google hasn't confirmed brand as a ranking factor), which feeds more brand visibility — a plausible compounding loop.
- In the AI era, mentions matter alongside backlinks, and AI assistants are becoming another SERP.
- Measure to justify: growth curves secure budget for the creative work you can't directly track.
