# The SEO Process in a Nutshell (Updated for AI Search)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
The SEO process is five steps: (1) get technicals right, (2) find a keyword, (3) create an optimized page, (4) build links, and now (5) optimize for AI search visibility. Classic fundamentals still drive everything, but AI assistants add new requirements: allow AI crawlers, build brand mentions, keep facts consistent everywhere, and write machine-readable content.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Get technicals right** — ensure crawlability and indexability (robots.txt, canonicalization, no stray blocks). Also check robots.txt, CDN rules, and server-level blocks for AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, etc.) — sites that want AI visibility should allow retrieval/search crawlers (opting out of model training is a separate business decision); being retrievable is how you appear in AI answers.
2. **Find a keyword to target** — prioritize by four criteria: traffic potential, business potential, ranking difficulty, and search intent (compromises are normal).
3. **Create an optimized page** — Google ranks on meaning, relevance, quality (on-page E-E-A-T + off-page links), usability, and context/settings. Match the SERP's dominant content type: a product page lost to listicles for "free seo tools," so the winning move was a listicle (now #1, ~1.3K visits/month) — if you list your own product, disclose it and rank the alternatives honestly.
4. **Build links** — internal links (crawl efficiency + link equity; e.g., sitewide navigation links funnel authority to key pages) and backlinks (best = followed, in main content, from relevant authoritative sites). Earn links with original data; build them with outreach (e.g., statistics roundup: research the most-cited stats, publish updated versions, ask linkers to outdated stats to switch). In the AI era, unlinked brand mentions matter too — they correlate strongly with AI brand recommendations.
5. **Optimize for AI search visibility**:
   - **Build brand mentions** — one correlation study of 75K brands: YouTube mentions top the list (~0.74), general branded web mentions next (0.66–0.71); domain authority ~0.27; backlink count negligible. Run quarterly mention-gap analyses vs. competitors.
   - **Keep brand info consistent** across LinkedIn, directories, Wikipedia, review sites — contradictions get stitched into wrong AI answers. Use Organization schema with `sameAs` links.
   - **Fill information gaps** — AI fabricates specifics when silenced; publish precise FAQs (including rumor denials), keep pricing/policies in plain HTML, own your comparison pages.
   - **Keep docs current** — outdated docs feed AI authoritative wrong answers; audit quarterly, publish changelogs, test what assistants say about your product monthly.
   - **Write for AI and humans** — headings, bullets, short sentences, schema (Product, Organization, Article), fast + mobile-friendly, minimal JavaScript-dependent content, allow AI crawlers.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Fix crawl/index issues; deliberately allow AI bots in robots.txt and CDN/firewall settings
- [ ] Score each keyword: traffic potential × business potential × difficulty × intent
- [ ] Match the SERP's dominant content type before writing
- [ ] Interlink new pages from high-authority pages; add sitewide links to cornerstone content
- [ ] Earn links with original data; build with targeted outreach
- [ ] Quarterly: brand mention gap vs. competitors; docs audit; AI-answer spot checks
- [ ] Standardize one canonical brand description across all third-party profiles
- [ ] Publish specific FAQs, plain-HTML pricing, and comparison pages

## Data points & benchmarks
- AI visibility correlations (one study, 75K brands): YouTube mentions ~0.737, web mentions 0.66–0.71, domain-level authority score 0.266, backlinks negligible.
- In a fabrication experiment (fake brand, 56 questions, 8 models), most models repeated planted fictions over the brand's own unspecific FAQ — specific fabrication beats vague truth.
- Only ~5.7% of new pages reached the top 10 for any keyword within a year (older study); in a survey of 4K+ practitioners, SEO typically took 3–6 months to show results.
- Example: matching content type to intent turned a losing product page into a #1 listicle drawing ~1.3K monthly visits.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Blanket-blocking AI crawlers "for safety" without a deliberate reason — it removes you from AI answers.
- Hiding brand facts behind JavaScript widgets — AI may not render them.
- Letting contradictory brand info live across directories — AI stitches mismatches into false statements.
- Assuming backlinks alone drive AI visibility — mentions and consensus matter more.

## Key principles
- SEO fundamentals haven't changed; AI visibility is layered on top of them.
- LLMs look for consensus: many sources mentioning you in the right context = trust.
- Silence about your own brand is a competitive risk — AI fills gaps with fabrications.
