# SEO vs. GEO: 5 Key Differences

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
SEO wins clicks from blue links; GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) wins mentions inside AI-generated answers. They share foundations (great content, intent, trust, crawlable site), but they differ in output, measurement, mention sources, and traffic quality. Strategy: build SEO as the foundation, layer GEO on top.

## Framework / Step-by-step
The five differences:
1. **Link vs. mention** — SEO places your page as a clickable result; GEO embeds your brand or facts inside the answer itself, where links are small, contextual, or absent.
2. **Click journey vs. zero-click** — with an AI summary present, users click a result only 8% of the time vs. 15% without (nearly 50% fewer); just 1% click the AI summary's sources; 26% end their session entirely after seeing an AI summary (vs. 16% in traditional results).
3. **Click economics vs. visibility economics** — SEO measures rankings, backlinks, organic traffic, share of voice. GEO measures AI mentions, citations, AI referral traffic, and AI share of voice — being seen and trusted even without a click.
4. **Your domain vs. third-party sites** — most AI mentions of a brand originate on third-party pages (industry rankings, "best of" lists, review platforms, PR), not the brand's own site. In AI Overviews, the top pages mentioning one well-known brand didn't include its own domain at all. Mentions don't need to be linked — unlinked references count.
5. **Browsing vs. intent** — AI-referred visitors view fewer pages (4 vs. 5.2), stay longer per page (86s vs. 78s), bounce more (67.8% vs. 63.7%), and land on homepages, product pages, and free tools rather than articles — they arrive with a specific goal.

Shared principles (GEO builds on SEO, not beside it): quality content still wins (in one study, a large site's content earned 7,470 AI mentions across 2,309 pages with no AI-specific optimization), user intent is unchanged, trust/brand authority matters in both (branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview presence; top-25% mention brands earn 10x+ more AI placements than the next tier), and crawlers still must be able to fetch and parse your pages (robots.txt respected; heavy JavaScript hurts AI more than classic SEO).

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Keep classic SEO healthy — it's the foundation AI systems learn from
- [ ] Add GEO levers: original data, quotable statistics, comparison ("X vs Y") pages, how-to guides, "best of" lists
- [ ] Pursue third-party mentions: review platforms, industry roundups, PR — even unlinked (earn them legitimately; disclose paid or affiliate relationships)
- [ ] Keep priority pages fresh; AI prefers citing newer content
- [ ] Structure content for machines: clear headings, Q&A blocks, bullets, schema
- [ ] Build multi-platform presence (video and community platforms are among the most-cited sources)
- [ ] Stand up GEO measurement: mention counts, citation share, AI referral traffic by page type

## Data points & benchmarks
- 8% vs. 15% click rates with/without AI summaries; 1% click AI-summary sources; 26% session end after AI summary (Pew study).
- AI visitors: 4 pages/visit vs. 5.2; 86s vs. 78s time on page; 67.8% vs. 63.7% bounce.
- AI referrals concentrated on free tools (~36%), product pages (~23%), homepage (~20%).
- Case: AI traffic was 0.5% of one brand's visits but drove 12%+ of signups (23x conversion lift vs. organic) — though uplift varies by site.
- Branded web mentions ↔ AI Overview presence correlation: 0.664; top-quartile mention brands get 10x+ more AI placements.
- ~3 in 10 users trust a leading chatbot more than search engines; 36% discovered a new product/brand through it (survey data).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Abandoning SEO to "do GEO" — AI models train and cite on the classic web.
- Measuring GEO with rankings/traffic only — visibility without clicks is the point.
- Ignoring third-party surfaces you don't control (lists, reviews, communities).
- Expecting AI traffic to behave like organic — it's sparse, intent-heavy, and lands on product/tool pages.

## Key principles
- SEO = click economics; GEO = visibility economics.
- In GEO, the web's opinion of you (mentions, reviews, lists) matters more than your own pages.
- Zero-click isn't zero-value: an accurate, trusted mention inside the answer can outperform a click.
- Start with SEO, then layer GEO: same content quality, intent focus, trust, and technical base.
