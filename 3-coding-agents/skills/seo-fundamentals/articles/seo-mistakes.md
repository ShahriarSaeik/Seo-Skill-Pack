# 15 Common SEO Mistakes (And How to Fix Them)

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
Most SEO failure comes from 15 recurring mistakes, roughly ordered by severity, including: blocking crawling/indexing, skipping keyword research, ignoring search intent, link shortages and link buying, treating SEO as one-time, mass-producing low-quality AI content, neglecting internal links and speed, schema spam, keyword stuffing, word-count obsession, lazy meta tags, and missing alt text.

## Framework / Step-by-step
The 15 mistakes and their fixes:

1. **Blocking crawling** — check robots.txt for `Disallow: /` rules; remove them so crawlers can reach content.
2. **Blocking indexing** — stray noindex tags kill rankings; crawl the site and remove noindex from pages that should rank.
3. **No keyword research** — random content targets nothing; 96.55% of pages get zero Google traffic (older study). Fix: seed keywords → keyword ideas report → pick relevant keywords with traffic potential.
4. **Not matching search intent** — the "backlink checker" fix (adding the free tool searchers wanted) drove +516% traffic in under six months. Study the SERP's dominant intent before creating.
5. **Not enough backlinks** — compare your page's links vs. top-ranking pages; replicate competitor link sources (e.g., pitch the same podcasts/interviews they appeared on).
6. **Buying backlinks** — risks manual action or algorithmic trust loss ("our algorithms lost trust with this website"). Fix: don't buy links.
7. **Treating SEO as one-time** — rankings are a competition; keep researching, creating, building links, staying technically sound, updating content.
8. **Scaling low-quality AI content** — short-lived wins (one case crashed after a month); "scaled content abuse" is officially spam policy since March 2024. Use AI as an assistant; add human value: Experimentation (original tests/surveys), Experience (first-hand proof), Effort (formats beyond plain text).
9. **Missing internal links** — they drive discovery, PageRank flow, and contextual understanding (anchor text). Find and add opportunities regularly.
10. **Slow pages** — Core Web Vitals are a ranking factor and ~70% of consumers said in one survey that page speed affects purchase willingness. Fix with caching, compression, CDNs, font and JS optimization.
11. **Schema markup spam** — marking up invisible/irrelevant content risks penalties and rarely works. Only mark up visible, accurate, original content.
12. **Keyword stuffing** — against spam policies; read unnaturally. Fix: cover subtopics comprehensively (use content gap on top-ranking pages to build H2 lists) instead of repeating keywords.
13. **Word-count obsession** — engagement drops past ~7 minutes reading time; in one dataset, traffic correlated positively with length only up to ~2,000 words, then negatively. Be comprehensive, not long.
14. **Under-optimized titles/meta descriptions** — among the most common technical issues on 1M+ domains audited. Fix missing, mismatched, too-long, or too-short tags.
15. **Missing alt text** — minor but free win; supports topical relevance and Google Images (a meaningful traffic source). Fix last — "don't major in minor things."

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Verify robots.txt and noindex tags aren't blocking anything important
- [ ] Build content only from keyword research with real traffic potential
- [ ] Confirm intent match (type/format/angle) before publishing
- [ ] Benchmark links vs. competitors; earn/replicate — never buy
- [ ] Run SEO as a continuous program, not a project
- [ ] Keep AI as an assistant; layer in original experiments, experience, effort
- [ ] Add contextual internal links to every important page
- [ ] Pass Core Web Vitals
- [ ] Keep schema honest and visible-only
- [ ] Replace stuffing with comprehensive subtopic coverage
- [ ] Set lengths by topic depth, not word-count targets
- [ ] Audit and fix all title tags/meta descriptions
- [ ] Backfill missing alt text

## Data points & benchmarks
- 96.55% of pages got no organic traffic (older study of the web at scale).
- Intent fix case: +516% traffic in <6 months after aligning page format with searcher expectations.
- ~70% of consumers admit page speed influences willingness to buy (older survey).
- Reading engagement declines past ~7 minutes (Medium data); >2,000-word posts show moderate NEGATIVE correlation with traffic.
- Meta tag issues rank among the top 15 most common technical SEO problems across 1M+ domains.

## Mistakes to avoid
- All 15 above — but prioritize crawlability and indexability first: nothing else matters if Google can't see you.
- Publishing AI output as-is: it cannot research, share experience, or argue opinions.
- Marking up schema for content invisible to users.
- Padding articles to hit arbitrary word counts.

## Key principles
- Fix order matters: crawlable → indexable → targeted → intent-matched → linked → fast → polished.
- Comprehensiveness beats length; relevance beats repetition.
- AI content is fine when it helps humans add value; scaled auto-generated content is spam.
