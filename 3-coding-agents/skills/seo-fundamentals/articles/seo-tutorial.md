# The SEO Tutorial That Even Your Mom Would Understand

- **Updated**: 2026-10-05

## TL;DR
SEO boils down to three steps: find what people are searching for (keyword research), give them what they want (match search intent + cover everything + show expertise), and prove to Google your page deserves to rank (internal links + backlinks). Simple doesn't mean easy — you're competing with everyone doing the same thing.

## Framework / Step-by-step
1. **Find what people are searching for (keyword research)**
   - Enter a broad industry word (e.g. "coffee") into a keyword research tool (free keyword generators typically show the ~100 most popular keywords containing the term).
   - Look at question-phrased keywords to see the most popular questions in the niche.
   - Note down keywords relevant to your business; discard irrelevant ones (e.g. TV shows, big brands).
   - Repeat with related seed words ("espresso," "latte," "cappuccino").
2. **Give them what they want (create matching content)**
   - Understand search intent — the reason behind the search. Use top-ranking pages as the proxy for intent (e.g. if all top results for "pour over coffee maker" are list-style blog posts, searchers are in research mode — don't try to rank a product page).
   - Cover everything searchers want to know — open top-ranking pages and note common subtopics (e.g. "how to grind coffee beans" pages all cover grinder-free methods; skipping that loses most searchers).
   - Showcase experience or expertise — write from first-hand testing/experience (e.g. actually test the coffee machines you recommend).
3. **Prove to Google it deserves to rank (links)**
   - Backlinks = "votes"; pages with more backlinks tend to rank higher. Google has said links are among its top ranking factors (2016 statement).
   - Internal links: add them from pages that already get traffic (crawl your site with an SEO audit tool and search page text for your target keyword to find high-traffic pages to link from), not just any page.
   - External links: earn them via genuine guest contributions (search `[topic] "write for us"` in Google, follow submission guidelines); paid or keyword-stuffed guest-post links break Google's link spam policies.

## Actionable checklist
- [ ] Brainstorm seed keywords that define your industry
- [ ] Run each seed through a keyword tool; collect the top 100 ideas + 50 questions per seed
- [ ] Filter out irrelevant keywords (brands, media, off-topic)
- [ ] For each target keyword, study top-ranking pages to identify content type/format (list, guide, product page)
- [ ] List subtopics covered by ALL top-ranking pages and include them
- [ ] Add proof of first-hand experience (test results, photos, original data, personal process)
- [ ] After publishing, find your own pages that mention the target keyword and add internal links
- [ ] Find guest blogging opportunities with `[topic] "write for us"` searches and pitch posts

## Data points & benchmarks
- Free keyword generators typically return ~100 keyword ideas per seed, plus the top ~50 question keywords.
- Google said in 2016 that links are among its top three ranking factors.
- Correlation: pages with more backlinks tend to rank higher (general rule, not a guarantee).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Trying to rank a product page where searchers want informational blog content (intent mismatch).
- Covering only the "obvious" answer and ignoring alternative methods/edge cases searchers actually want.
- Adding internal links from irrelevant or zero-traffic pages.
- Buying or trading links, or mass guest-posting purely for anchor-text links.
- Expecting SEO to be fast — it takes time and sustained effort; if impatient or unwilling to grind, SEO may not be for you.

## Key principles
- "SEO isn't complicated: find what people are searching for, give it to them, prove to Google it deserves to rank."
- Google ranks what helps searchers — relevance to actual intent beats optimization tricks.
- Write from experience or expertise; people want to learn from those who actually did the thing.
